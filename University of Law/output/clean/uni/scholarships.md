## International Bursaries

The University of Law offers the International Bursary to eligible international students. To be an international student you must have:

- a confirmed place on a course listed in the International Bursary Terms and Conditions;
- be a non-British national;
- be someone who does not have a settled or pre-settled status; and
- be an overseas fee payer.

There is no separate application process for the International Bursary. If you are eligible for the International Bursary you will be notified of your entitlement in your offer letter.

All eligible international students will receive the International Bursary annually and the amount will depend on which course you are studying. The International Bursary is awarded as an annual course fee reduction.

For information about the International Bursary or to find out more about any available international scholarships, please email [internationalscholarships@law.ac.uk](mailto:internationalscholarships@law.ac.uk).

- If you were issued with an offer for a qualifying course commencing between 1 June 2026 and 31 May 2027, please see the relevant International Bursary Terms and Conditions .

#### European Bursary

The University of Law is offering the European Bursary to eligible students who are domiciled in a qualifying country, who apply to study an On Campus face-to-face on one of our eligible postgraduate courses commencing on intakes between September 2026 and May 2027.

#### Who is eligible for the European Bursary?

To be eligible for the European Bursary you must:

- be domiciled in a qualifying country listed in Schedule 1 of the European Bursary Terms and Conditions;
- be classified as a European Student as per the European Bursary Terms and Conditions;
- have a confirmed place on a qualifying postgraduate course to start between 1 September and 2026 and 31 May 2027; and
- be an overseas fee payer.

There is no separate application process for the European Bursary. If you are eligible for the European Bursary, you will be notified of your entitlement in your offer letter.

#### Enhanced International Bursary

The University of Law is offering the Enhanced International Bursary to eligible students for their first academic year only. This bursary is offered in addition to the International Bursary for students who are domiciled in a qualifying country, who apply to study an On Campus face-to-face on one of our eligible undergraduate courses commencing in intakes between September 2026 and May 2027.

#### Who is eligible for the Enhanced International Bursary?

To be eligible for the Enhanced International Bursary you must:

- be domiciled in, i.e. live, in a one of the countries listed Schedule 2 the International Bursary Terms and Conditions;
- have a confirmed place on a qualifying undergraduate course to start between 1 September and 2026 and 31 May 2027; and
- be an overseas fee payer.

There is no separate application process for the Enhanced International Bursary. If you are deemed eligible for the Enhanced International Bursary, this will be applied as a Discretionary Discount for the first academic year only and viewable on your Applicant Portal.

#### Regional Scholarships

The University of Law is offering 500 Regional Scholarships to applicants who are domiciled in a qualifying country, who apply to study an On Campus face-to-face on one of our eligible postgraduate courses commencing in intakes between September 2026 and May 2027.

#### Who is eligible for the Regional Scholarship?

To be eligible for the Regional Scholarship you must:

- be domiciled in, i.e. live, in a country listed in Schedule 2 of the Regional Scholarship Terms and Conditions;
- have accepted your place and enrolled on a qualifying postgraduate course to start between 1 September and 2026 and 31 May 2027; and
- be an overseas fee payer.

There is no separate application process for the Regional Scholarship. If you are eligible for the Regional Scholarship, you will be notified of your entitlement via email. The Regional Scholarship is subject to availability.
