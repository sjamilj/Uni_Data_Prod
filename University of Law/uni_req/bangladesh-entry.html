                      Top-Up Degree

                      English Language Requirements
                      An English language level equivalent to IELTS 6.0 or above with a minimum of 5.5 in each component.

                      Entry Requirements
                      Level 5 HND or equivalent qualification at Level 5 in a Business related subject. Students that possess an acceptable qualification in non-business related area (e.g. HND Law and Practice, HND Engineering, HND in Computing) will be considered providing they meet the following criteria:

                      • obtained a final classification of Merit or Distinction
                      • submit a full CV along with one professional reference
                      • Have a minimum of two years proven work experience in a business environment

                      Please note: Applicants who already hold a Degree or Masters programme in a Business-related subject are not eligible for this top-up course

                      Postgraduate Entry Requirements

                      Law

                      Postgraduate Diploma in Law

                      9 months

                      English Language Requirements
                      An English language level equivalent to IELTS 6.5 or above with a minimum of 6.0 in each component.

                      Entry Requirements
                      A Master's degree from Bangladesh (completed following a 2-4 year Bachelor's degree) or a Bachelor's degree (BUET only) or Bachelor's of Dentistry or Surgery (from specific Universities) – 60% or GPA 3.0 or B grade or a 2:2 or above from a UK Bachelor's degree in any discipline.

                      Legal Practice Course (Accelerated, Full and Part)

                      6 months, 9.5 months and 22 months respectively

                      English Language Requirements
                      An English language level equivalent to IELTS 6.5 or above with a minimum of 6.0 in each component.

                      Entry Requirements
                      2:2 or above from a UK Qualifying Law degree or a pass at the PGDL

                      MA Law

                      9 months

                      English Language Requirements
                      An English language level equivalent to IELTS 6.5 or above with a minimum of 6.0 in each component.

                      Entry Requirements
                      A Master's degree from Bangladesh (completed following a 2-4 year Bachelor's degree) or a Bachelor's degree (BUET only) or Bachelor of Dentistry or Surgery (from specific Universities) – 65% or GPA 3.25 or B+ grade or a 2:2 or above from a UK Bachelor's degree in any discipline.

                      LLM

                      One year

                      English Language Requirements
                      An English language level equivalent to IELTS 6.5 or above with a minimum of 6.0 in each component.

                      Entry Requirements
                      A 4 year Bachelor's degree from Bangladesh – 60% or GPA 3.0 or B grade or a 2:2 or above from a UK Bachelor's degree in any discipline

                      Bar Practice Course

                      10 months

                      English Language Requirements
                      An English language level equivalent to IELTS 7.5 or above with a minimum of 7.5 in each component or Pearson level 78.

                      Entry Requirements
                      2:1 or above from a UK Qualifying Law degree or a commendation or distinction from the Postgraduate Diploma in Law (PGDL).
                      You will also be required to pass the Bar Standards Board aptitude test.

                      Business

                      Business Master’s Degrees

                      One year

                      English Language Requirements
                      An English language level equivalent to IELTS 6.0 or above with a minimum of 5.5 in each component.

                      Entry Requirements
                      A 4 year Bachelor's degree from Bangladesh – 60% or GPA 3.0 or B grade or a 2:2 or above from a UK Bachelor's degree in any discipline*
                      * For the MSc Global Accounting, your degree should be in an area which demonstrates the understanding of numbers (for example Accounting, Finance or Business with Accounting/Finance).

                      Entry Requirements

                      Before joining us you’ll need to look at the entry requirements for the course you’re hoping to study and confirm you hold the necessary qualifications from your country.

                      Select your country from the dropdown menu to see our course subject areas list and the minimum entry requirements for each qualification.

                      English language requirements 

                      Take a look at our list of accepted English language qualifications to see if you meet our requirements. Don’t worry if you don’t hold one of the qualifications listed, we’ll offer you The University of Law English Test (ULET) so you can study with us.

                      Contact us

                      If you are unable to see the country you’re from in our list or have any questions regarding our courses and the application process, please email our Admissions team on admissions@law.ac.uk for language and entry requirements information specific to your country.

                      Due to restrictions imposed by the UK Government, we are not currently accepting applications from the following countries, should you require a Student Route visa; Afghanistan, Cameroon, Sudan, Myanmar. You can find more information here.
