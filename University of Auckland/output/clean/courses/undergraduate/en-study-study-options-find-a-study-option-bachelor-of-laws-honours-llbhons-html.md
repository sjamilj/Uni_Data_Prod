# Bachelor of Laws (Honours) LLB(Hons)

## Quick facts

- **Duration:** Full-time: 4 years
- Part-time: Varies

- **Next start date:** 2028 Semester One – 28 February

- **Available locations:** City

- **Points:** 480

- **Programme type:** Undergraduate
- Conjoint options available

## 2027 entry requirements

### Programme requirements

There is no direct entry to the LLB(Hons) programme for students who have completed their prior study overseas. Entry is by invitation for existing high-achieving LLB Part III students.

International students wishing to study the LLB(Hons) should first successfully complete Parts I and III of the LLB programme to the required level at Auckland Law School.

You could be eligible for [another programme](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option.html), or check out these alternative pathways:

- Bridge your educational gap with a preparation and foundation programme .
- If you have an offer of place from us that is conditional on meeting English language requirements, you may be considered for English Pathway courses from the University of Auckland English Language Academy (ELA)

## How much does a Bachelor of Laws (Honours) cost per year?

### 2027 fees

- **International students:** NZ$51,024 *

Fees are set in advance of each calendar year and will be updated on this website. Fees are inclusive of 15% GST, but do not include the Student Services Fee, course books, travel and health insurance, or living costs. Amounts shown are indicative only. In addition to the tuition fees, there is a Student Services Fee of $9.44 per point, estimated at $1,132.80 for full-time study (120 points). Fees will be confirmed upon completion of enrolment into courses.

*Please note: amounts shown are indicative and estimates only.

[See course fees for each faculty](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/fees-and-money-matters/tuition-fees/fees-by-faculty.html)
