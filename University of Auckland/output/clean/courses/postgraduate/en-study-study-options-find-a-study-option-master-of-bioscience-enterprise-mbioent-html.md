# Master of Bioscience Enterprise MBioEnt

## Quick facts

- **Duration:** Full-time: 1 year
- Part-time: Varies

- **Next start date:** 2027 Semester One – 1 March
- 2028 Semester One – 28 February

- **Available locations:** City

- **Points:** 120

- **Programme type:** Postgraduate
- Research options available

## 2027 entry requirements

### Programme requirements

You must have completed the requirements for the [Postgraduate Diploma in Bioscience Enterprise (PGDipBioEnt)](/en/study/study-options/find-a-study-option/postgraduate-diploma-in-bioscience-enterprise-pgdipbioent.html) from this University with a Grade Point Average of 6.0 or higher.

This is a limited entry programme as per the [Limitation of Entry Statute 1991](/assets/about-us/about-the-university/the-university/official-publications/university-calendar/current-calendar/2025-calendar-parts/2025-calendar-academic-statutes-regs.pdf).

n/a

If you do not meet the above entry requirements, but have other relevant experience and think you would be successful in postgraduate study, please contact us to discuss alternative pathways into our programmes.

## How much does a Master of Bioscience Enterprise cost per year?

### 2027 fees

- **International students:** NZ$58,258 *

Fees are set in advance of each calendar year and will be updated on this website. Fees are inclusive of 15% GST, but do not include the Student Services Fee, course books, travel and health insurance, or living costs. Amounts shown are indicative only. In addition to the tuition fees, there is a Student Services Fee of $9.44 per point, estimated at $1,132.80 for full-time study (120 points). Fees will be confirmed upon completion of enrolment into courses.

*Please note: amounts shown are indicative and estimates only.

[See course fees for each faculty](https://www.auckland.ac.nz/en/study/fees-and-money-matters/tuition-fees/fees-by-faculty.html)
