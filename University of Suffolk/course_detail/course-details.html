Overview

The Certificate of Higher Education (CertHE) in AI and Automation Practitioner (Higher Apprenticeship) is designed for individuals who want to develop practical skills in artificial intelligence, automation, process improvement, and digital transformation.

As organisations increasingly adopt AI-enabled technologies, there is growing demand for professionals who can identify opportunities for automation, improve business processes, and support organisational change. This apprenticeship combines academic learning with workplace application, enabling you to develop knowledge and skills that can be applied directly within your organisation. Through a blend of face-to-face teaching, online learning, and workplace-based activities, you will learn how to analyse business processes, design AI-enabled solutions, evaluate their effectiveness, and support continuous improvement. The course is suitable for both new entrants and existing employees who wish to develop their digital capabilities and contribute to organisational innovation and efficiency.

On completion of your apprenticeship you will be awarded a CertEd Artificial Intelligence and Automation Practitioner (Higher Apprenticeship) from the University of Suffolk.

The University of Suffolk has an international profile while being committed to our region. We are proudly modern and innovative and we believe in transformative education. We are on the rise with a focus on student satisfaction, graduate prospects, spending on academic services and student facilities.

Course Modules

Downloadable information regarding all University of Suffolk courses, including Key Facts, Course Aims, Course Structure and Assessment, is available in the Definitive Course Record.  

This module introduces you to organisational processes, stakeholder engagement, and process mapping techniques. You will analyse workflows, identify inefficiencies, and recognise opportunities for process improvement and automation within workplace settings.

This module focuses on designing and developing AI-enabled and automated solutions. You will explore workflow design, automation tools, low-code and no-code platforms, solution prototyping, and the practical application of AI technologies within organisations.

This module develops skills in testing, evaluating, and improving automation solutions. You will learn how to assess solution performance, identify issues, ensure reliability and compliance, and apply continuous improvement techniques.

This module examines the implementation and adoption of AI-enabled solutions within organisations. Topics include change management, stakeholder engagement, user adoption, performance measurement, and continuous improvement

Following successful completion of the on-programme learning and Gateway requirements, apprentices will complete the Apprentice Assessment in accordance with the AI and Automation Practitioner Apprenticeship Standard.

Entry Requirements

The University will liaise with employers regarding entry requirements, but would normally expect Apprentices to meet our standard entry requirements:

48 UCAS Points (or above)

  • DD (A-Level)
  • PPP (BTEC)
  • Pass D or E (T Level)
How to Apply Entry Requirements Explained

All applicants under 19 are required to hold GCSE English and Maths at Grade C/4 or above (or Level 2 equivalents).

All applicants must be employed for more than 30 hours per week in a suitable role and have the support of their employer.

IELTS 6.0 overall (minimum 5.5 in all components) where English is not the students' first language.

Career Opportunities

This apprenticeship prepares you for a range of roles involving digital transformation, business improvement, and AI-enabled technologies. Potential career pathways include:

  • AI and Automation Practitioner
  • Process Improvement Practitioner
  • Digital Transformation Officer
  • Business Improvement Analyst
  • Automation Analysis
  • Operations Improvement Coordinator
  • Digital Project Support Roles
  • Business Systems Support Roles

The skills developed through the course are transferable across a wide range of sectors including business services, healthcare, public sector organisations, manufacturing, logistics, education, and technology. The programme also provides a foundation for progression to higher-level study in digital business, computing, data analytics, and related disciplines.

Our Careers, Employability and Enterprise Team are here to support you, not only whilst you complete your studies, but after you graduate and beyond.

To find out more about our range of services and support, please visit our Careers, Employability and Enterprise page.

Apprenticeship Funding

Degree Apprenticeships are only offered to students undertaking the course as part of their employment. Apprenticeship training will therefore be funded directly by employers or by employers in partnership with Government. Apprentices will not be required to pay any tuition or course fees, and will not be eligible for any Student Finance England maintenance support. Please see our Apprenticeship pages for more information.
