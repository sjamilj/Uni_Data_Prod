### Welcome to Suffolk

### Accepted Qualifications

The University of Suffolk welcomes applications from students with a wide range of qualifications. Please refer to the information below for details on our accepted qualifications, including English language requirements.

If you have any questions, please contact our Admissions Team .

For entry onto an undergraduate course, applicants are required to have one of the following qualifications:

- Bangladesh Technical Education Board Diploma at 60% (or other recognised institution e.g. paramedic, nursing or midwifery)
- 2 year bachelor's degree at 60%
- International Foundation Programme
- International Baccalaureate Diploma with 28 points
- A-Levels (see course pages for requirements)

If you are studying, or have studied, an INTO International Foundation Programme, please see our [INTO International Foundation Programme Qualifications](https://www.uos.ac.uk/media/uniofsuffolk/website/content-assets/documents/international/017-INTO-Entry-Requirements_02-2026_A3-digital-[Final-ac].pdf).

For entry onto a postgraduate course, applicants are required to have one of the following qualifications:

- 4-year bachelor's degree with 2.75 GPA
- 2 year master's degree with 50%

If English is not your first language, you may need to provide evidence of your English language ability as part of your application.

The most commonly known assessments for this that we accept are;

- IELTS (Academic/UKVI)
- Cambridge English
- Pearson (Academic)
- TOFEL (iBT)

Please see our [English Language page](https://www.uos.ac.uk/international/english-language-requirements/) for further details of requirements for your course.

## Agents

The University of Suffolk works with education agents, also known as representatives, to support prospective students with their application to study abroad.

They can help provide guidance on our courses and entry requirements, as well as the wider University. They can also assist you in submitting your application to study with us.
