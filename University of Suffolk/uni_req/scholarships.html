Fees and Scholarships

We understand that making a choice about how to finance your education in the UK is a big decision. The University of Suffolk offers competitive tuition fees and a number of scholarships designed to support your academic aspirations.

Our Ipswich Campus is only an hour away from London and Cambridge, making the University of Suffolk a great location for any international student looking for a thriving town that won't break the bank.

For those studying at our London Centre in Southwark, you'll benefit from being in the heart of the capital while still enjoying access to the same academic and financial support.

International Tuition Fees

As one of the top ten universities in the UK for our facilities and career prospects in the WhatUni Student Choice Awards 2023, you'll be getting a high-quality education at an affordable price.

  2026-2027
Undergraduate (BA, BSc) £15,900–£18,870
Postgraduate Taught (MA, MSc) £16,020–£18,900
Postgraduate Research (PhD,MPhil) See PhD webpage for the latest fees

International students can apply for a payment plan that allows annual tuition fees to be paid over two instalments. Further information is available in our Tuition Fee Policy.

Please check your course page for the latest tuition fees for your chosen year of study.

Scholarships 

International Scholarships 2026-2027

The University of Suffolk International Merit Scholarship (2026/27) is a scheme designed to reward international students who meet or exceed our published entry requirements (based on the country where you obtained your qualifications). The scheme is open to applicants of undergraduate and postgraduate-taught programmes delivered by the University of Suffolk at the Ipswich campus or the London Centre. 

The awards are £1,000 - £3,000 based on your academic achievements and level of study.

Important information

  • Undergraduate students will be awarded the International Merit Scholarship in each year of study (excluding sandwich/placement/study abroad years - see terms and conditions below)
  • This is an automatic scholarship - scholarship awards will be notified to you via email once an offer of study has been made
  • Scholarships are awarded as fee discounts

This scheme has been developed to support and encourage alumni students to progress to full or part-time postgraduate taught or research programmes at the University.
