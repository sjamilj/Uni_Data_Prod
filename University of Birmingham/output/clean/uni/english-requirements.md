# International entry requirements

If English is a requirement of your offer, we offer [Presessional English](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/international/bia/presessional/eap-presessional-new.aspx) courses all year round as an alternative route into University of Birmingham study. Presessional English courses are designed for students who wish to improve their knowledge of spoken and written English in preparation for academic study. You can take a Presessional English course as an alternative to retaking IELTS or a similar qualification.
