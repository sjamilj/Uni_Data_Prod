International entry requirements

We welcome undergraduate applications from all candidates offering A level or equivalent overseas qualifications (including the International Baccalaureate or a recognised Foundation Programme). All candidates must achieve appropriate grades in their examinations.

Academic requirements

The A level requirements are listed under the 'entry requirements' tab on the relevant course page in our course finder.

To find out what the grade requirements would be for a qualification that you have studied in your own country, please select your country from the drop-down list on the relevant course page, or see your individual country page for information.

English language requirements

In order to undertake a programme of study here at the University, you will need to demonstrate that you have a suitable level of English proficiency.

You can demonstrate your level of English with any of the qualifications stated below. The sections below will tell you what grades you need in these qualifications for the subject area you want to study.

English tests/qualifications normally need to have been taken within two years of the start date of your programme.

Proof of English language proficiency

For those joining us for 2026, we will accept the following additional tests as evidence of English language proficiency:

  • TOEFL iBT or iBT Home Edition
  • IELTs Academic, IELTS Academic Online or IELTS for UKVI
  • PTE or PTE Academic Online
  • Cambridge English
  • Language Cert ESOL SELT (UKVI)
  • Trinity ISE II
  • Trinity ISE III
  • Language Cert Academic
  • Oxford Internatinal ELLT Global or ELLT Digital

We accept IELTS One Skill Retakes.

Please note: we are only able to accept your most recent English language proficiency test score to meet our requirements.

English language courses

If English is a requirement of your offer, we offer Presessional English courses all year round as an alternative route into University of Birmingham study. Presessional English courses are designed for students who wish to improve their knowledge of spoken and written English in preparation for academic study. You can take a Presessional English course as an alternative to retaking IELTS or a similar qualification.

Alternative qualifications for undergraduate programmes

Please note that the grades stated above are the University minimum and some degree programmes will have higher requirements. Always check your offer letter for full details of your conditions. 

If exams in your country are cancelled and this affects the final qualification you receive from your school, the University of Birmingham will consider suitable alternatives. We will confirm what these suitable alternatives are after confirmation has been received from the Government or Education Ministry in your country that exams have been cancelled, and what qualification matriculating students will receive.
