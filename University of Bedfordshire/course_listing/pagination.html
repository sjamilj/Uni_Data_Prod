    Psychology, Crime and Justice BSc (Hons)

    Please note that this course has changed its name for 2025 entry; it was previously called Forensic Psychology BSc (Hons).

    Our accredited course brings to life the exciting field of forensic psychology, exploring how it can help us understand the minds of offenders, criminal behaviours and why people commit crimes, with reference to specific crimes. You also focus on crime investigation and what happens in the courtroom in areas such as eyewitness behaviour; detecting deceptive behaviours; and exploring how juries reach their verdicts. In your final year, you develop the advanced skills needed to assess offenders and put in place interventions.
