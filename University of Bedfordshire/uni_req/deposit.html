Tuition fee deposit

The initial tuition deposit for international students at the University of Bedfordshire is 65% of the total tuition fee before the university will issue a Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS).

The tuition fee pre-payment of 65% will be calculated on the full, published tuition fees and will not take into account any scholarships, bursaries or awards. These will be applied to the remaining balance of your fees.
