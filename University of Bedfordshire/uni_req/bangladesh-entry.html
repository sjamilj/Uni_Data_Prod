Bangladesh

Luton has a vibrant Bangladeshi and south Asian community and this extends to our vibrant capital city of London which is only 22 minutes away on the direct train from Luton. Having the local community around you gives you the support and understanding of home with the excitement and career-building opportunities that the University of Bedfordshire offers. From local events such as the Mela festival, to many local religious centres and groups, the University of Bedfordshire is your Home from Home. Our student union also has a very active Bangladeshi society which you are welcome to be part of.

Fees & Scholarships

The University of Bedfordshire has a number of generous scholarships available to international students. For the latest information, visit our Fees & Scholarships page.

Our High Achievers Undergraduate Scholarship for Bangladesh is worth £3000 off international tuition fees for each year of study. To receive the award, the below criteria must be met. Applicants must be:

  • Classed as International/Overseas fee-payer status. Home fee paying students are not eligible.
  • Achieve 4.8/5 or more in the Bangladeshi Year 12 exams
  • Applying for the first year of an undergraduate course at level 4 (first year). Applicants looking to enter at level 5 or 6 are not eligible as they would not be entering on the basis of an eligible qualification
  • Students who are successful in being awarded the bursary will not be eligible for any other scholarships or bursaries, but will be eligible for the prompt payment discounts on tuition fees.

Our Postgraduate Global Merit Scholarship is worth £3000 off the total international tuition fees for the course. To receive the award, the below criteria must be met. Applicants must be:

  • Classed as International/Overseas fee-payer status. Home fee paying students are not eligible
  • Applying for a postgraduate course between September 2024 and July 2025

If you would like to apply direct, please find the course you are interested in, go through the course options (where you are applying from, when you want to start etc.) and then click on the 'Apply now' button.
