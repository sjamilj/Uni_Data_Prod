Vice-Chancellor's International Excellence Scholarship

Source URL: University of Waikato scholarship page

2027 award value: NZ$8,000, credited towards tuition fees for students commencing in 2027. Payment is conditional on meeting requirements stated in the Formal Offer of Place.

Study levels supplied: Foundation, Undergraduate and Postgraduate (taught), across all subject areas.

Eligibility

  • New international student enrolling full-time at the University of Waikato.
  • Conditional or unconditional offer of admission.
  • Minimum GPA equivalency of B+ in the previous qualification used for entry.
