Foundation Years at Cardiff Met

View Courses
01 - 01

What We Offer

Our foundation years are integral to most of our undergraduate degrees. They act as a year 0 and after successful completion you can automatically progress to the first year of your chosen degree without reapplying*.

They offer you the chance to strengthen your skills, knowledge and build confidence in the relevant area of study. They are a great choice if you don’t meet the requirements or don’t have the relevant qualification to enter year one of a chosen degree, or if you are returning to learning after a prolonged period of time.

* Conditions apply

01 - 01

A foundation year gives you access to all the opportunities across the entire university experience. You can apply for accommodation, engage in all extra-curricular activities and benefit from the same dedicated support available to all undergraduate students.

Opportunities for exploration

“When I first enrolled in this programme, I knew I wanted to be an engineer, I just didn’t know what kind. I wanted a career that would challenge me, allow me to help people, and give me room to be creative. With the opportunities for exploration provided in the foundation year, I know I’ll find exactly that.”
