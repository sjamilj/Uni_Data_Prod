Accepted Secure English Language Tests (SELTs)

All SELTs must be taken from a UKVI approved secure test centre and be dated within 2 years of the course start date. A list of test centres can be found at Prove your English language abilities with a secure English language test (SELT) - GOV.UK.

 

Qualification Courses with 6.0 IELTS Requirements Courses with 6.5 IELTS Requirements

IELTS Academic

We accept IELTS One Skill Retake

 Overall score: 6.0 (with no component below 5.5) 6.5 overall with 6.5 in both reading and writing, and a minimum of 6.0 in the remaining components
Pearson Test of English Academic (PTE Academic) Overall score: 59 (minimum score required in each skill area is 59) Overall score: 62 (minimum score in reading and writing 62, speaking and listening 59)

TOEFL IBT (Internet-based test) – Home Edition is not accepted

We accept TOEFL IBT MyBest Score

Test taken AFTER 21 January 2026:
Overall score: 4. Minimum scores required: 4 in all skills

Test taken BEFORE 21 January 2026:
Overall score: 72. Minimum scores required: Reading: 18, Listening: 17, Writing: 17, Speaking: 20

Test taken AFTER 21 January 2026:
Overall score: 4.5. Minimum scores required: Reading: 4.5, Writing: 4.5, Listening: 4, Speaking: 4

Test taken BEFORE 21 January 2026:
Overall score: 83. Minimum scores required: Reading: 19, Listening: 20, Writing: 24, Speaking: 20
Cambridge English Advanced (CAE) Overall score of 169 with no component score lower than 162 C1 is 180-199, upper middle would be 195+, lower middle would be 185+
Trinity College London

Test taken AFTER May 2026:
ISE 90 overall with minimum 80 in each skill

Test taken BEFORE May 2026:
ISE II pass with minimum merit in each skill

Test taken AFTER May 2026:
ISE 96 overall with minimum 90 in each skill

Test taken BEFORE May 2026:
ISE II pass with distinction in each skill / ISE III pass with pass in each skill
LanguageCert Academic SELT (ONLINE NOT ACCEPTED) Overall score of 65 with no score lower than 60 Overall score of 70 with minimum 70 in both reading and writing and minimum 65 in speaking and listening
PSI Skills for English: SELT (Skills for English: Global is NOT accepted) Pass with merit overall and pass in each sub-test in the B2 level test Pass with merit overall and pass with merit in all sub-tests
