# International Scholarships

Scholarships for International Students studying at Cardiff Met.

​​We believe all students deserve the opportunity to enhance their career prospects, irrespective of their financial situation. That’s why we offer a range of undergraduate and postgraduate scholarships for our international students.

All International applicants will be automatically considered for our scholarships and will not be expected to complete a separate application. Scholarships are confirmed to applicants at point of offer and full details of scholarship eligibility and exceptions can be found in the Terms & Conditions below.

Cardiff Metropolitan University is offering a new EU Scholarship* for eligible undergraduate students from selected EU countries, making it more affordable than ever to study in the UK.

If you start an undergraduate degree at Cardiff Met in September 2026, you'll automatically receive the scholarship, reducing your annual tuition fees from £16,000 to £9,790. This means you'll pay the same tuition fees as UK students throughout your degree.

*Available to students from Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Italy, Latvia, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden.

To be eligible for a scholarship, applicants must accept their offer by the deposit deadline.

Undergraduate, Alumni, MBA and Vice Chancellor Scholarships, please note:

- Students are entitled to one scholarship only and not eligible to receive multiple scholarships from Cardiff Metropolitan.
- The Vice Chancellor Scholarship applies to only PG taught courses with £17,600 fee. Other specialist courses do not qualify
- The Alumni Scholarship is available to Cardiff Metropolitan undergraduate degree holders including students from our TNE partnership institutions who study a master’s on our Cardiff campus. For full details and eligibility please visit this page .

Our international student scholarships do not apply to:

- If you are sponsored by your company, government or similar, you will not be entitled to the international scholarship.
- Where an applicant has had credit exemption reducing the amount of credits studied and fees payable.
- Courses of less than one year in length.
- Subject to availability
- Subject to change - please check the information regularly to ensure you are aware of the current procedures and deadlines
- Our Scholarships are awarded to FULL fee-paying International Students ONLY. Should your fee status change from International to Home (UK), you will not be eligible for the scholarship awarded by the University.
- Students must be studying full-time, on campus to qualify for the scholarship. Distance Learning students are not eligible
- No cash alternatives will be offered, and the scholarship cannot be transferred to any other course at Cardiff Met or to any other nominated recipient.
- We cannot guarantee the scholarship for deferred entry and deferred applicant will be subject to the scholarship levels and fees in place for the next intake period.
- If an applicant is allocated a scholarship but then withdraws their application or does not enrol onto the course for any reason, the scholarship is forfeited.
- The university reserves the right to withdraw or not award scholarships at any point before they are allocated.
- You must have applied by the relevant deadline for the intake to be considered for the scholarship (unless there is a different deadline specified on the application form) – applications after this date may not be considered.
