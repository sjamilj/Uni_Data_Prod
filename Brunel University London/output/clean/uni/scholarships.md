# Scholarships and bursaries

## For financial help with your studies, take a look at our scholarships, bursaries and discounts.

We offer a range of scholarships to our offer holders. You’ll need to complete an application for some of our scholarships and for others you’ll be automatically considered.

### Do you have a question about scholarships?

Contact the scholarships team at [scholarships@brunel.ac.uk](mailto:scholarships@brunel.ac.uk) or check out our [Scholarship FAQ page.](https://www.brunel.ac.uk/study/admissions/FAQs/Scholarship-FAQ)

## Important information regarding scholarships, bursaries and Brunel University of London discounts

If you are eligible for more than one scholarship or bursary, you will be awarded the scholarship or bursary with the highest monetary value. Please check each terms and conditions page below for further information on the criteria. If you are applying for a scholarship or discount and have been asked to submit evidence along with your application, please do so in a timely manner. If your application is incomplete, the scholarships team will not be able to assess your application. Each individual terms and conditions will advise you on how evidence should be submitted.
