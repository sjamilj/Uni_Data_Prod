International Foundation Programme (4-year degree programme)

Study a comprehensive programme leading to a range of degrees at the University of East London on one integrated visa for the entire duration of your degree.

About the programme

The International Foundation Programme is your first step toward a wide range of undergraduate degrees at the University of East London. You’ll study on one integrated visa for the full duration of your degree, beginning with a comprehensive foundation year designed to build your academic skills and subject knowledge.

The programme combines a set of core modules with subject-specific modules tailored to your chosen progression degree stream. This structure allows you to strengthen essential academic skills while beginning to specialise in your field of interest.

Core modules include:

  • English for Academic Purposes (EAP)

  • Research and Study Skills

  • Mental Wealth: Professional Life

Key facts

Minimum English language level:

Academic IELTS 5.5 overall (with no component below 5.5)

Academic entry requirements:

Students need to achieve GCSE/iGCSE with minimum grades of 44444 (or equivalent), which is approximately equivalent to 5 subjects at grade C.
 
Each application will be considered individually and assessed on their ability to succeed on the International Foundation Programme.

Minimum age requirement:

September start: 16 years of age. All students must turn 17 years of age by 31 December 2026.
January start: All students must be 17 years of age.
