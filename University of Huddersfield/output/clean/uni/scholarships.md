# International Scholarships

Search text crawl start

Each year, we welcome students from around the world to our diverse and inclusive community. To help support the cost of studying, we offer a range of scholarships for eligible international students, including merit-based, subject-specific and research scholarships. These are available for both undergraduate and postgraduate study.

## Merit-based scholarships

At the University of Huddersfield, we recognise academic excellence by offering a merit-based scholarship of up to £4,000. There is no specific application process for these scholarships.

Once you apply to study at the University as an international student, you will automatically be considered for this scholarship if you meet the eligibility criteria. If you’re awarded a scholarship, you will be notified in your offer letter and given the full details of what you will receive.

For international undergraduate students, you could be awardedup to £4,000for each year of your course, based on your academic achievements as follows.

### Eligibility criteria

To be eligible for our merit-based scholarship, you must:

- be an international fee-paying student who is self-funding their studies, meaning you are not already receiving any funding
- be applying for a full-time taught undergraduate, postgraduate or PGCE course (unless you are already qualified for a bursary)
- **for postgraduate courses,**  you must have achieved a  **minimum 2.2 or equivalent**  in your undergraduate degree and must meet the English and academic entry requirements for your course.

### Terms and conditions

- Students will be notified in their offer letter if they have been awarded a scholarship and what the full details are.
- Eligible international students will be awarded the scholarship for every year of their course, excluding their placement year.
- This scholarship will not be awarded alongside any other University or external scholarships.

### Exclusions

The following students are not eligible for the merit-based scholarships.

- International students applying for the MA TESOL with Placement.
- Master’s with placement students in the Huddersfield Business School are not eligible for these scholarships.
- Master’s by Research programmes are not eligible for merit-based scholarships. Please see our Research Scholarshipsfor available opportunities when applying for a research degree.
- Applicants with refugee, asylum-seeker status, or indefinite leave to remain in the UK are not eligible.

## Chevening Scholarships

This prestigious and highly competitive award is a full scholarship covering your tuition fees and living expenses.

Chevening Scholarships are the UK government’s global scholarship programme, funded by the Foreign and Commonwealth Office (FCO) and partner organisations. The programme makes awards to outstanding scholars with leadership potential from around the world to study postgraduate courses at universities in the UK.

The Chevening programme was established in 1983 and has developed into a prestigious international scheme. Chevening Scholars come from more than 150 countries and territories worldwide (excluding the USA and the EU), and this year the Scholarships will support more than 1,500 individuals. There are over 43,000 Chevening alumni around the world who together comprise an influential and highly regarded global network.

- The programme provides full or part funding for full-time courses at postgraduate level, normally a one-year Master’s degree, in any subject and at any UK university.
- These scholarships can not be awarded in conjunction with the University of Huddersfield Scholarships for new international undergraduate and Master's students.

Full information including how to apply is available at on the Chevening Scholarships page .
