LSBU Future Global Graduate Awards
In recognition of the continued commitment of international students to London South Bank University, we are proud to launch the LSBU Future Global Graduate Awards.
The awards are given in the form of a tuition fee discount to international students on acceptance of their offer and receipt of their deposit by a set deadline. Students may only accept one of the awards.
The Vice-Chancellor’s International Merit Award
The Vice Chancellor’s International Merit Award recognises outstanding academic achievement of students obtaining grades equivalent to AAB and above at UK A-level (approximately 75% or above in other international school leaving certificates) and comprises an award of £2,000 for each academic year.
The Global Success Scholarship
The second award, The Global Success Scholarship, is worth up to £4,000 and is awarded to international students to provide them with a springboard to kickstart their global career at LSBU, meaning the practical career-focused courses provide even greater value. 
It’s available to students for their first year of study at LSBU.
ScholarshipValueEligibility
The Vice-Chancellor's International Merit AwardUndergraduate
£2,000 per annum.		Grades equivalent to AAB and above at UK A-level (approximately 75% or above in other international school leaving certificates) and not in receipt of any other award from LSBU.
The Global Success Scholarship
Undergraduate and postgraduate
A range of awards up to £4,000, varying by subject area, at both Undergraduate and Postgraduate levels.
Open to all international students on acceptance of the offer and payment of the early deposit amount by the set deadline.
The awards allow students to fast-track their student visa to enable them to start their studies on time.
To be eligible, you must be an international student fee payer at undergraduate or postgraduate level, meet the conditions of your offer and complete payment of your deposit (£2,000) within four weeks of meeting all the conditions.
The awards are designed to ensure that all international students can benefit from the high-quality, career-focused education that London South Bank University offers.
We have also introduced three new scholarships at London South Bank University (LSBU) to support international students in their academic journey. The European Impact Scholarship, LSBU Global Partner Scholarship, and CEG Progression Award provide financial assistance and recognise outstanding potential, reinforcing our commitment to fostering global connections. These initiatives ensure talented students have the resources they need to thrive and succeed at LSBU.
ScholarshipValueEligibility
European Impact Scholarship
Undergraduate and postgraduate
£5,000 tuition fee discount per academic year for the entire duration of their undergraduate or postgraduate taught programme.
To qualify, applicants must:
  • Be new self-funded international fee-paying students from Europe.
  • Enrol in a standard undergraduate (UG) or postgraduate taught (PGT) programme at LSBU.
  • Have successfully completed a recognised European pre-university qualification.
  • Verify that your country is eligible by checking the following table.
Transnational Education (TNE)
Partner Award
£5,000 tuition fee discount per academic year automatically applied to eligible students joining us from one of our recognised global partner institutions.
To qualify, you must:
  • Be a new self-funded international fee-paying student.
  • Be enrolling on a standard full-time undergraduate (UG) or postgraduate taught (PGT) programme at LSBU.
  • Have completed your most recent qualification at a recognised LSBU TNE partner institution within the past two years.
CEG Progression Award
Undergraduate and postgraduate
£5,000 tuition fee discount for the first year of undergraduate or postgraduate taught study.
To qualify, you must:
  • Be a new self-funded international fee-paying student.
  • Be progressing directly from an LSBU pathway programme delivered by Cambridge Education Group (CEG).
  • Hold a standard CAS or iCAS, and be enrolled on a standard UG or PGT programme at LSBU.
  • Begin your LSBU degree immediately after completing your pathway qualification.
Accommodation discount (subject-specific)75% discount from Accommodation fees at McLaren House for students joining us in select areas such as Engineering, Construction, STEM, and based on the region.
To be eligible, you must:
  • Be a new self-funded international fee-paying student.
  • Be enrolling on a standard full-time undergraduate (UG) or postgraduate taught (PGT) programme at LSBU.
  • Pay your full fees up front by the deadline given.
  • Be fully enrolled by the course start on 26th January (this includes your UK arrival and any online onboarding tasks).
Please note that regional and subject-specific exclusions apply for this discount.
LSBU Pathway Progression Scholarship£5,000 discount from headline gross tuition fees for the first year of study. Available to new self-funded international fee payer, on a standard UG or PGT programme progressing to LSBU immediately upon completing their pathway qualification. Not applicable to Study Abroad, Short Courses, Postgraduate Research (PGR), or other non-standard programmes. Not permitted to be used alongside any other scholarship.
 
Available to students progressing from an LSBU-recognised pathway provider delivering an Ofqual-regulated Level 3 Foundation or, where approved, Level 4 International Year One (IY1) qualification. 
 
Progression from IY1 routes is subject to academic mapping and course-level approval. Alternatively, be progressing from a recognised partner or articulation institution as recognised by the LSBU Global & LSBU International Team.
Eligible providers include, but are not limited to:
  • NCC Education
  • INTO Study
  • David Game College
  • Kaplan International Pathways
  • Study Group
  • Oxford International Education Group
  • NCUK
  • Official LSBU articulation and progression partners as confirmed by LSBU Global & LSBU International Team
Applicants must also meet LSBU’s academic and English language entry requirements.

How to apply
External scholarships for US students
Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship 
 The Benjamin A. Gilman International Scholarship offers thousands of scholarships for students who are eligible for Federal Pell Grants at their home institution. The deadline for Spring students is in early October. The deadline for Summer and Fall students is in March. 
BUTEX International Scholarship 
 As a BUTEX affiliated university we are able to offer you the opportunity to apply for the £500 BUTEX International Scholarship. The Scholarship is open to all International (non-EU) Study Abroad students who are not funded by an ERASMUS grant. To be eligible to apply you must have been offered a study abroad place here. The deadline for applying for this scholarship is mid-June every year. 
Chevening Scholarships
 The UK Government's global scholarship programme is funded by the Foreign and Commonwealth Office (FCO) and partner organisations. The Scholarships offer nationals from a variety of countries highly coveted opportunities to study for a Master's degree at a UK university. They can develop their skills, create new networks, strengthen their opportunities and expand their horizons. Application information is on the Chevening website. 
Foundation for Global Scholars
 The Foundation for Global Scholars attempt to inspires and equip young adults to tackle global challenges through international experience, leadership skills, and cultural competence. They offer various scholarships. 
Fund for Education Abroad 
 The Fund for Education abroad are committed to increasing the opportunities for dedicated American students to participate in high-quality education abroad programmes. They offer many scholarships for various amounts. 
Go Overseas 
 Each semester, Go Overseas offers scholarships to Study Abroad students based on the creativity and analytical thinking demonstrated through writing samples and/or video submissions. 
Tortuga Backpacks
 Tortuga Backpacks believe that students deserve to experience the world beyond their college campuses, but appreciate that not everyone can afford to travel. Funds may be used for tuition, accommodation, books, or travel. Deadlines are in May and December.
