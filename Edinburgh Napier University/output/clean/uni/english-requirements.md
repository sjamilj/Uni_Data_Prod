## Accepted English language tests

We accept the following English language tests. The grades below are the minimum requirementsand some courses require a higher level. Visit our course listings for further information. If you hold a language certificate that is not listed below, please get in touch with us.

[Browse our courses](https://www.napier.ac.uk/courses/study-areas)

You’re very welcome to apply before taking an English language test. If your application is successful, but we need further evidence that you meet our English language requirements, an English language test will likely be a condition of your offer. The test would need to be completed less than 2 years before your course start date.

### UKVI Approved Secure English Language Test (SELT)

Please note, in some cases offers will require you to complete a UKVI approved Secure English Language Test (SELT). If this is the case, only the below tests are accepted. If you are unsure if this applies to you, please consult your offer letter

#### IELTS for UKVI Academic (from approved test centre)

6.0 overall, with no component below 5.5

IELTS One Skills Retake is accepted if you do not meet the required score in one component but it must be taken within 60 days of your full IELTS test. More information can be found on the [IELTS FAQs.](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/booking-your-test/one-skill-retake#FAQs)

#### LanguageCert English language qualification (test centre test only, online invigilated tests are not accepted)

65 overall with no component below 60

#### Pearson’s Test of Academic English

59 overall with a minimum of 59 in each component

#### PSI Service (UK) Skills for English (taken outside the UK)

Skills for English UKVI B2 Level - Pass in all 4 skills

#### Trinity College London - Integrated Skills in English (taken in UK only)

ISE II 90 overall with minimum 80 in each skill

### Undergraduate and postgraduate taught degrees

#### IELTS (Academic)

6.0 overall, with no component below 5.5

IELTS One Skills Retake is accepted if you do not meet the required score in one component but it must be taken within 60 days of your full IELTS test. More information can be found on the [IELTS FAQs.](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/booking-your-test/one-skill-retake#FAQs)

Entry to our Pre-sessional English for Academic Purposes Programme requires an IELTS Academic for UKVI test from an approved test centre.

#### TOEFL Internet Based Test

80 overall with a minimum of 17 in listening, 18 in reading, 20 in speaking and 17 in writing (if test taken prior to January 2026)

4 overall with a minimum of 4 in listening, 4 in reading, 4 in speaking and 4 in writing  (if test taken from January 2026)

We are no longer accepting Home Edition or Paper tests.

#### Pearson’s Test of Academic English

59 overall with a minimum of 59 in each component

#### Cambridge C2 Proficiency (formerly Cambridge English Proficiency (CPE))

169 overall with a minimum of 162 in each component

#### Cambridge B2 First (formerly Cambridge English First (FCE))

169 overall with a minimum of 162 in each component

#### Cambridge C1 Advanced (formerly Cambridge English Advanced (CAE))

169 overall with a minimum of 162 in each component

#### Trinity Integrated Skills Test (ISE)

ISE II 90 overall with minimum 80 in each skill
