Accepted qualifications and English tests

  • Accepted qualifications
  • Pre-sessional English
  • Tests Online
  • Tests in China
  • Contact us

We accept a range of English language qualifications as evidence of your abilities. These qualifications are all checked by our International Centre for English to ensure they meet our requirements.

Their syllabuses are mapped against the four components of listening, reading, writing and speaking to ensure they are comparable to CEFR level B2 in each of these components.

For the majority of our courses, an IELTS of 6.0 overall (with no band below 5.5) is required, however, please note that some courses may require a higher IELTS score. Please check with the University if you are unsure.

Important information to note:

  • Qualification expiry dates

    Some of these qualifications may have a validity of two years from the date of the test.

  • Exceptions

    If you have a UK Bachelor Degree or an equivalent qualification that was studied in a majority English speaking country, depending on the course you have applied for to study with us you may not be required to hold one of these English language qualifications.
