English Language Requirements

To join the University of Greenwich as an international student, alongside your academic qualifications, you will also need to meet the university’s English language requirements, which vary depending on the course you have applied for.

There are several ways that you can do this, such as holding a passport from certain countries, completing certain degrees in English, having done English as part of your high school qualifications, or completing an English language test. You can find out the specific qualifications that we can accept on the dropdowns below.

  • Below are the English language qualification requirements

  • IELTS

    The most widely available and commonly held English language qualification by international students is the IELTS test, run by the British Council. If you do not hold a valid English language qualification before you apply to study with us, any offer we make will be conditional on you achieving a required IELTS score. IELTS One Skill retake is accepted. Please note the expiry date is based on the original test not the retake.

    The score you need depends on the course you have applied for. The requirements by faculty are set out in the tables below, with any exceptions included as individual courses.

  • Alternative English exemptions

    • If you are a national of a majority English speaking country (as defined by UKVI) you can be exempted from providing proof of English proficiency
    • If you have previously completed a full bachelor's or master's degree entirely in English in a majority English speaking country (as defined by UKVI) then you can be exempted from providing proof of English proficiency
    • If you have previously completed a degree from a UK university (either in full or as a top up) then you can be exempted from providing proof of English proficiency

  • Pre-Sessional English course

    If you already took an IELTS test but did not achieve score we require, we offer Pre-Sessional English programmes, which helps you to ‘top up’ your IELTS score. We offer various options depending on the score you have achieved and the level your course requires. See the requirements for the programme.

    Please note if you want to study a pre-sessional English course, if your IELTS has any skills below 5.5, it must be a UKVI approved IELTS test in order for us to accept you onto our pre-sessional English programme. A UKVI approved test is required by the UKVI in order to sponsor a visa application for any pre-sessional English programme.

  • English language test partner

    We have teamed up with our long-term academic partner, Oxford International Education Group, to offer students the chance to take an online entry test in place of an IELTS, if you are not able to take one.

    This test can be taken online and is available for students who hold an offer to study with us. The score you require from this test relates to the IELTS requirement of your chosen programme of study, listed above. The table below shows the required score you must meet from the Oxford ELLT.

    Once you have completed the Oxford ELLT, the result will be emailed to you directly, and you must forward the result to us at international@gre.ac.uk to confirm your requirement has been met.

    Please note that if you are a national of Bangladesh, then you are not able to take this test as an IELTS waiver.

    IELTS 6.0 with 5.5 in each skillOIETC 6 overall, minimum 5 in each skill
    IELTS 6.5 with 5.5 in each skillOIETC 7 overall, minimum 5 in each skill
