The University of Greenwich is a place with no limits on what you can achieve. For over 130 years we have been helping students to explore their talents and reach their personal and academic goals. We are a friendly and welcoming community where international students make up around 30% of our student population. So, whichever campus you are on, you’ll soon feel at home. Plus, all our student accommodation is open to international students.

We’ll be by your side with whatever you need, ensuring that you’re cared for and supported while you’re a student with us. If you’re eligible, you can apply for a scholarship or bursary, contributing up to £5000 towards your tuition. You could also earn money by taking on part-time work at the university through our Student Ambassador Scheme.

Whatever you’re interested in, you’ll find a course that’s right for you. We offer career-focused degrees in many subject areas, including business, law, architecture, computing, design, education, health, engineering, science, humanities and social sciences. You’ll find our standard entry requirements for most courses below. Some courses may have different entry requirements so please visit the relevant course page for more details.

Why choose Greenwich?

Located in historic campuses in London and Kent, we provide industry-relevant courses that are accredited by professional bodies. You'll benefit from our modern study facilities, including advanced laboratories and libraries.

Academic entry requirements

  • Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) with a minimum GPA 4.0 (80%) or above
  • Completed Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) and achieved 60% or above in a Polytechnic Diploma

For some courses, you will need to have studied relevant subjects at school/college to be eligible. Our admissions team will determine this once you apply.

English language entry requirements

For details on English language requirements, please see our English Language Requirement page.

Pre-sessional English programmes are only available to students that have completed a UKVI IELTS. Please visit our Pre-sessional English course page for more information.

There is no OIETC waiver for Bangaladesh nationals.

For certain programmes such as Psychology, Law, and Architecture, a higher English language proficiency is required. Please see our English Language Requirement page for further information.

Academic entry requirements

For entry to our Master's programmes, you will need to have met the following:

  • Four-year bachelor degree with a minimum of 2.5 for UK 2:2 or a minimum of 2.8 for UK 2:1

For some courses, you will need to have studied relevant subjects at university to be eligible. Our admissions team will determine this once you apply.

How to apply

Undergraduate courses

International applicants who wish to study at undergraduate (Bachelor‘s) level can either apply through one of our trusted agents below or direct to us via UCAS (University and Colleges Admissions Service).

Postgraduate courses

International applicants who wish to study at postgraduate (Master’s) level with us can either apply directly using our online application form or through one of our one of our trusted agents below.
