## Pre-degree

Please see below our range of bursaries for our Access to Higher Education students for 26/27 academic year. The online application forms will go live at the start of August 2026. Please note that no funding can be paid until you have fully enrolled on course.

Students in receipt of an[Advanced Learner Loan](https://www.gov.uk/advanced-learner-loan/how-to-apply)from Student Finance England to fund their level 3 Access to Higher Education course, may be eligible for support from the advanced learner loan bursary fund. The money can help pay for things like:

- travel
- course materials and equipment
- childcare.

This funding is provided by the ESFA and therefore, strict eligibility criteria apply.

Eligibility

Students must be in receipt of an advanced learner loan and have a household income of £35,000 or less.

How to apply

Students can apply for an advanced learner loan bursary by completing the on-line application form and provide the Bursaries team with:

- A copy of your advanced learner loan statement
- Personal bank statements for the past 3 months
- A completed student budget planner ( available online or from the student services team).

And one of the following as proof of your family's household income. If you are over 25, we will require evidence of your annual income:

- a letter from the department of work and pensions showing receipt of benefits
- a P60 from the last tax year
- a tax credit award notice
- copies of online statements for universal credit (last 3 months).

Students applying for childcare support should download the[childcare provider form](https://www.ravensbourne.ac.uk/asset-bucket/prod/2020-07/Advanced-Learner-Loans-Bursary-Childcare-Provider-Form-20_21.pdf)which needs to be completed by the childcare provider.

All supporting evidence should be emailed to[bursaries@rave.ac.uk](mailto:bursaries@rave.ac.uk)

Student Finance England will ‘write off’ any outstanding Advanced Learner Loan balance you owe against your Access to HE course, once you complete and Graduate from an undergraduate degree course.

The higher education course must be[eligible for student finance](https://eur01.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.gov.uk%2Fstudent-finance%2Fwho-qualifies&data=05%7C02%7Cwebsite%40rave.ac.uk%7Cce03ae9dde3b45a350bb08de123258e9%7Cde280afaf4144fb9b8228b01edd08554%7C0%7C0%7C638968204615422615%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=AA2apj4ll48eyhtgCpZcsDA4zPnDdBe3H3M%2FLha8dSE%3D&reserved=0).

If you made extra repayments towards your Advanced Learner Loan before starting a Higher Education course, these repayments are non-refundable.

The Pre Degree Hardship Bursary provides additional support to Access to HE students who are facing unforeseen financial difficulties.  Awards are made based on individual need assessment.

About

The Pre Degree Hardship Bursary is not a loan and does not need to be repaid. Support is available to assist with living and course equipment costs.

There is no guarantee that any applicant will be eligible for or awarded a bursary.

Awards are usually paid in 2 termly instalments. All applications will be assessed for a full academic year.

The award is for maintenance only; no award will be made for tuition fees nor payment made to students with fees outstanding

We will notify you of the decision on your application within 20 working days of receipt of your application, providing all details and full supporting evidence are supplied. If any evidence is missing, the decision on your application will be delayed.

Eligibility

The Pre Degree Hardship Bursary is available to Access to HE students, who:

- are enrolled to study at the Greenwich Peninsula Campus
- are not eligible to support from the Advanced Learner Loan Bursary
- can demonstrate that they made realistic provision at the start of the course to fund both the course tuition fees and living costs for the duration of the course.

How to apply

Students are asked to complete the on-line application form* and supply copies of the following evidence:

- Bank statements for past 3 months
- Tenancy agreement (if applicable)
- Weekly/monthly payslip (if applicable)
- Completed student budget planner
- Personal bank statements for the past 3 months
- A completed student budget planner ( available online or from the student services team).

And one of the following as proof of your family's household income. If you are over 25, we will require evidence of your annual income:

- a letter from the department of work and pensions showing receipt of benefits
- a P60 from the last tax year
- a tax credit award notice
- copies of online statements for universal credit (last 3 months).

All evidence should be sent to[bursaries@rave.ac.uk](mailto:studentservices@rave.ac.uk)

Ravensbourne will check attendance, academic progress and engagement as part of our assessment process.

Depending on individual circumstances, students may also be required to meet with a member of the Finance Support Team to discuss additional forms of support and money management.

* online application form will go live on 1-9-2026 for September 2026 intake.

## Undergraduate - 2026/27

In addition to any government loans you may be eligible for, Ravensbourne University London has a number of bursaries available to help support our undergraduate students.

Students with a household income of up to £35,000, will receive an automatic cash award of £500 per academic year of study.

Eligibility

The Ravensbourne bursary is available to home/UK students, who:

- are studying a undergraduate degree course in 2026/27 academic year.
- are paying home tuition fees of £9,790.
- have an approved means assessed maintenance loan from the Student Loans Company.
- have a household income of £25,000 or less (Home undergraduate students only) OR
- have a household income of £35,000 or less, as declared to SFE (Home undergraduate students only) AND meet one of the following criteria: - are under 25 and a care-leaver as declared to SFE (home undergraduate students only)
- are under 25 and irreconcilably estranged from family as declared to SFE (home undergraduate students only)
- are a mature student (over 25 on first day of course)
- with disabilities and chronic medical conditions which may affect paid employment (home undergraduate students only)
- have carer responsibilities (home undergraduate students only)
- who live in a low University participation area (home undergraduate students only)

Please note: Eligibility is restricted to students paying the full Home undergraduate tuition fee of £9,790 . Students paying a lower fee are not eligible for the Ravensbourne Bursary.

How to apply

Students do not need to apply as they will automatically be assessed for eligibility against our criteria when they apply for Student Finance with the [Student Loans Company](https://www.slc.co.uk/).

When will it be paid?

Eligible Year 0, 1 & 2 (level 3, 4 & 5) students will receive £500 on or around 5 February 2027 following successful progression to Semester 2.

Year 3 (level 6) students will receive £500 cash on 27 November 2026 following confirmation of attendance on course.

Please note:

- The Ravensbourne bursary is only paid once per course level. Students repeating a year will not be eligible to receive the award again.
- Attendance and engagement on course may be taken into consideration.

[The Amazon Future Engineer bursary](https://raeng.org.uk/amazon-future-engineer-bursary)is a national bursary programme aimed at supporting women A-level and BTEC/OCR (or Scottish equivalent) students from low-income households who wish to study computer science or related engineering courses at UK universities.

Accelerating the careers of women in Engineering

Women are still significantly underrepresented in engineering and technology in higher education. UCAS data on university application and acceptance figures for the 2020 cycle1 highlighted that women represent just 16% and 18% of accepted applications to computing and engineering degrees respectively. At the current rate of progress, parity of women on engineering degrees will not be achieved until 2085. We cannot wait that long.

Awardee benefits

These bursaries aim to help address underrepresentation and accelerate the rate of progress. They will provide successful women applicants with a financial support package worth £5,000 per year for up to four years of study to cover expenses related to attending university, including tuition fees or accommodation and living costs.

By removing some of the financial barriers that may impact on university studies and career trajectory, recipients can remain committed to their courses and stand the best possible chance of success.

Awardees will also gain exclusive access to networking groups to meet positive role models, interact with like-minded peers, build long-term relationships, and benefit from collaboration opportunities.

The Royal Academy of Engineering is committed to diversity and inclusion and welcomes applications from all under-represented groups across engineering. It is the Academy's policy to ensure that no applicant is disadvantaged or receives less favourable treatment because of age, disability, gender reassignment, marriage and civil partnership, pregnancy and maternity, race, religion or belief, sex or sexual orientation. We would like to support you to achieve a balance between your personal and work demands. We are happy to discuss individual requirements and consider part time and other flexible working arrangements.

For more information please contact[Lauren Pattle](mailto:lauren.pattle@raeng.org.uk) at The Royal Academy of Engineering.

- Evidence of any benefits received (if applicable)
