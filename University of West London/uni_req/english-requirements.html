      Introduction

      If you are from a country that UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) have not defined as majority English speaking, you will need to provide evidence of your language ability to study at the University.

      If you are a national of a majority English speaking country or have completed an undergraduate degree in one of these countries, you may be exempt from submitting an English language test. 

      Please refer to the list of countries classified by UKVI as majority English speaking below.

      Why we need English language requirements

      You may be able to receive a conditional offer if you apply without an English test, however you will not be able to enrol until the English language requirement has been met. Applicants requiring a Student Route Visa must have met all the conditions listed in their offer, including the English language requirement, in order to request a Confirmation of Acceptance of Studies (CAS) statement from UWL. 

      See below for a list of acceptable English language tests and standard scores by entry point, along with information on qualifications our Admissions team may accept in place of an English test.  

      Further information on qualifications that may be accepted to demonstrate English language abilities can be found on our country pages.

      Minimum scores can vary, please visit our course pages to view the specific English Language requirement for that programme.

      A Secure English Language Test (SELT) is required to join one of our Foundation courses.

      English language requirements for international students

      Please note we only accept the English tests listed below. Your English test must be valid on the first day of the course.

      • Cambridge B2 First / C1 Advanced

        • Integrated Foundation Year courses – Overall score of 162 (minimum of 162 in all components)
        • Undergraduate courses – Overall score of 169 (minimum of 162 in all components)
        • Enhanced Extended Masters courses - Overall score of 169 (minimum of 162 in all components)
        • Extended Masters courses - Overall score of 176 (minimum of 169 in all components)
        • Masters courses - Overall score of 176 (minimum of 169 in all components)

        Learn more about Cambridge B2 First and C1 Advanced

      • Duolingo

        Please follow the instructions Duolingo have provided here to share your results with us. 

        If you achieve the required scores, we will invite you for a video call before removing the English language condition from your offer.

        • Integrated Foundation Year courses – Overall score of 100 (minimum of 100 in each individual subscore)
        • Undergraduate courses – Overall score of 105 (minimum of 100 in each individual subscore)
        • Enhanced Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 105 (minimum of 100 in each individual subscore)
        • Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 120 (minimum of 100 in each individual subscore)
        • Masters courses – Overall score of 120 (minimum of 100 in each individual subscore)
        • PhD courses – Overall score of 120 (minimum of 100 in each individual subscore)

        *Duolingo English Tests must be issued from July 2024, and be dated within 24 months of your course start date. All tests that predate July 2024 are not accepted. 

        Learn more about Duolingo.

      • IELTS Academic

        IELTS tests must be taken at a UKVI Approved Test Centre. Please see the list of Approved Test Centre locations from the IELTS website.

        • Integrated Foundation Year courses – Overall score of 5.5 (minimum of 5.5 in all components)
        • Undergraduate courses – Overall score of 6.0 (minimum of 5.5 in all components)
        • Enhanced Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 6.0 (minimum of 5.5 in all components)
        • Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 6.5 (minimum of 5.5 in all components)
        • Masters courses – Overall score of 6.5 (minimum of 5.5 in all components)
        • PhD courses – Overall score of 6.5 (minimum of 6.0 in all components)

        Learn more about IELTS

      • LanguageCert Academic

        • Integrated Foundation Year courses – Overall score of 60 (minimum of 60 in all components)
        • Undergraduate courses – Overall score of 65 (minimum of 60 in all components)
        • Enhanced Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 65 (minimum of 60 in all components)
        • Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 70 (minimum of 60 in all components)
        • Masters courses – Overall score of 70 (minimum of 60 in all components)
        • PhD courses – Overall score of 70 (minimum of 65 in all components)

        Learn more about LanguageCert

      • Pearson PTE Academic

        • Integrated Foundation Year courses – Overall score of 51+ (51+ in all components)
        • Undergraduate courses – Overall score of 51+ (51+ in all components)
        • Enhanced Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 56+ (51+ in all components)
        • Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 58+ (51+ in all components)
        • Masters courses – Overall score of 58+ (51+ in all components)
        • PhD courses – Overall score of 58 overall (55+ in all components)

        Learn more about Pearson PTE

      • Skills for English SELT: UKVI Speaking, Listening, Reading and Writing at B2

        • Integrated Foundation Year courses – Overall result pass (with min pass in each unit)
        • Undergraduate courses – Overall result pass (with min pass in each unit)
        • Enhanced Extended Masters courses – Overall result pass (with min pass in each unit)
        • Extended Masters courses – Overall result pass with merit (with min pass in each unit)
        • Masters courses – Overall result pass with merit (with min pass in each unit)

        Learn more about Skills for English

      • TOEFL iBT

        • Integrated Foundation Year courses – Overall score of 4 (minimum of 4.0 in all skills)
        • Undergraduate courses – Overall score of 4 (minimum of 4.0 in all skills)
        • Enhanced Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 4 (minimum of 4.0 in all skills)
        • Extended Masters courses – Overall score of 4.5 (minimum of 4.5 writing, speaking and one other skill, and minimum of 4 in the remaining skill)
        • Masters courses – Overall score of 4.5 (minimum of 4.5 writing, speaking and one other skill, and minimum of 4 in the remaining skill)
        • PhD courses – Overall score of 4.5 (minimum of 4.5 writing, speaking and one other skill, and minimum of 4 in remaining skill)
        TOEFL iBT must be taken at a test centre. Home Edition is not an accepted test for entry to our online pre-sessional or degree programmes.
         
        • Learn more about TOEFL iBT

      Exemptions

      Applicants with the following qualifications (if taken within the last 5 years) may be exempted from providing an English language certificate:

      • GCSE/IGCSE English Language as a First or Second Language grade C/4 or above
      • IB English HL grade 4+ or SL grade 5+.
      • High School Diploma with English score of 70% or above awarded by an institution located in a majority English speaking country (please see the list below).
      • Undergraduate degree awarded by institution located in a majority English speaking country (please see the list below).
      • Foundation Programme (RQF level 3) awarded by a UK university.
      • INTO IFY English for Academic Purposes (EAP) module with an overall score of 50%+ and 40%+ in each individual skill component (for entry to undergraduate courses with IELTS requirement of 6 overall with 5.5 in each component).

      Please refer to our country pages for information on other qualifications that may be considered for exemptions

      We may accept the following English language qualifications from students with EU Settled / Pre-settled status. In addition, we will take into account any of your previous studies in English, for example at IB, foundation or undergraduate level. The levels listed here are an indication only and we will consider your application on its own merit.

      Please note these are not applicable if you require a Student Route Visa to study in the UK.

      • Possible exempting qualifications for EU Settled / Pre-settled applicants

        • Bulgarian Diploma of Completed Secondary Education – Grade 5 or above in English language
        • Cambridge PET – Pass with distinction
        • Cambridge FCE – Pass at Grade C minimum
        • Cambridge Advanced / Proficiency / Business Higher / Legal / Financial – Pass
        • Danish Bevis (HF/HHX/HTX/STX) – Grade 7 or higher
        • ESOL Skills for Life – Levels 1 & 2​
        • French Baccalaureate – Grade 6 (HL) or Grade 12 (OIB) 12-20
        • German Abitur – Mark 'Gut' at Abitur 11
        • Linguaskill – Overall average of 169 or higher (with no less than 162 in each component)
        • Lithuanian Brandos Atestatas – Grade 8 at B2 level
        • Michigan Language Test – B2 in all components
        • Password Skills – Overall score of 6, with no less than 5.5 in any section
        • Polish Extended Matura – 70%+
        • Portuguese Certificado de fim de Estudos Secundários / Diploma Nível Secundário de Educação – Grade 14 or higher
        • Track Test – B2 Level
        • Trinity Integrated Skills in English – ISE II, ISE III, ISE IV
        • Romanian Baccalaureate – B2 Level in all elements
        • Slovakian Maturia – B2 Level
        • Swedish Upper Secondary School – VG/C or B/6 minimum

      Majority English speaking countries

      The UK government classifies the following countries as majority native English speakers:

      • Europe

        Ireland

        Malta

        United Kingdom

      • North and Central America

        Antigua and Barbuda

        The Bahamas

        Barbados

        Belize

        Canada*

        Dominica

        Grenada

        Jamaica

        St Kitts and Nevis

        St Lucia

        St Vincent and the Grenadines

        United States of America

        *Applicants presenting Canadian qualifications will need to provide evidence from the institution that the degree was taught and examined in English.

      • Oceania

        Australia

        New Zealand

      • South America

        Guyana

        Trinidad and Tobago
