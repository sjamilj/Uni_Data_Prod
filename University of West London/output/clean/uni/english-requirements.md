# English language requirements

## Introduction

If you are from a country that UK Visas and Immigration (UKVI) have not defined as majority English speaking, you will need to provide evidence of your language ability to study at the University.

If you are a national of a majority English speaking country or have completed an undergraduate degree in one of these countries, you may be exempt from submitting an English language test.

[Please refer to the list of countries classified by UKVI as majority English speaking below](https://www.uwl.ac.uk/international/your-country/english-language-requirements#countries).

## Why we need English language requirements

You may be able to receive a conditional offer if you apply without an English test, however you will not be able to enrol until the English language requirement has been met. Applicants requiring a Student Route Visa must have met all the conditions listed in their offer, including the English language requirement, in order to request a Confirmation of Acceptance of Studies (CAS) statement from UWL.

See below for a list of acceptable English language tests and standard scores by entry point, along with information on qualifications our Admissions team may accept in place of an English test.

Further information on qualifications that may be accepted to demonstrate English language abilities can be found on our [country pages](https://www.uwl.ac.uk/international/your-country).

Minimum scores can vary, please visit our [course pages](https://www.uwl.ac.uk/courses) to view the specific English Language requirement for that programme.

A [Secure English Language Test (SELT)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt) is required to join one of our Foundation courses.

## Exemptions

Applicants with the following qualifications (if taken within the last 5 years) may be exempted from providing an English language certificate:

- GCSE/IGCSE English Language as a First or Second Language grade C/4 or above
- IB English HL grade 4+ or SL grade 5+.
- High School Diploma with English score of 70% or above awarded by an institution located in a majority English speaking country (please see the list below).
- Undergraduate degree awarded by institution located in a majority English speaking country (please see the list below).
- Foundation Programme (RQF level 3) awarded by a UK university.
- INTO IFY English for Academic Purposes (EAP) module with an overall score of 50%+ and 40%+ in each individual skill component (for entry to undergraduate courses with IELTS requirement of 6 overall with 5.5 in each component).

[Please refer to our country pages for information on other qualifications that may be considered for exemptions](https://www.uwl.ac.uk/international/your-country)

We may accept the following English language qualifications from students with EU Settled / Pre-settled status. In addition, we will take into account any of your previous studies in English, for example at IB, foundation or undergraduate level. The levels listed here are an indication only and we will consider your application on its own merit.

Please note these are not applicable if you require a Student Route Visa to study in the UK.

- **Cambridge PET –** Pass with distinction
- **Cambridge FCE –** Pass at Grade C minimum

- **Danish Bevis (HF/HHX/HTX/STX) –** Grade 7 or higher
- **ESOL Skills for Life –** Levels 1 & 2​
- **French Baccalaureate –** Grade 6 (HL) or Grade 12 (OIB) 12-20
- **German Abitur –** Mark 'Gut' at Abitur 11

- **Lithuanian Brandos Atestatas –** Grade 8 at B2 level
- **Michigan Language Test –** B2 in all components
- **Password Skills –** Overall score of 6, with no less than 5.5 in any section
- **Polish Extended Matura –** 70%+
- **Portuguese Certificado de fim de Estudos Secundários / Diploma Nível Secundário de Educação –** Grade 14 or higher
- **Track Test –** B2 Level
- **Trinity Integrated Skills in English –** ISE II, ISE III, ISE IV
- **Romanian Baccalaureate –** B2 Level in all elements
- **Slovakian Maturia –** B2 Level
- **Swedish Upper Secondary School –** VG/C or B/6 minimum
