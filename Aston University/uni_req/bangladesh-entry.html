Aston University is one of the leading career-focused universities in the UK, dedicated to providing real-world experience to all of our students.

Located in the centre of Birmingham, the UK's second-largest city, Aston is a welcoming home for career-minded students. The 2025 Longitudinal Education Outcomes (LEO) data shows that employed Aston University graduates are among the highest paid in the country, earning on average £33,600 three years after graduation.

International Foundation Programme

Students who are not eligible for undergraduate studies starting Year 1 may apply for a one-year International Foundation Programme and then progress to an undergraduate programme. After successfully completing a pathway programme, students are guaranteed entry to an undergraduate programme (Integrated CAS Route) at Aston University.

Alternatively, you could also explore our International Year One pathway with OnCampus for progression to Year Two if your grades are marginally under our direct entry requirement.

The list of programmes you can progress to at Aston University can be found here.

For detailed eligibility criteria for International Foundation Programme and the International Year One progression routes, please visit the OnCampus entry requirements page

Benefits of a pathway programme at ONCAMPUS Aston include the chance to:

  • Improve English language abilities
  • Develop academic and study skills, such as writing, reading and presentation
  • Understand how to be successful in a UK university
  • Develop further research study skills at master's level
  • Receive specialised university progression support


ONCAMPUS Aston takes place within Aston University's campus so students can enjoy all of the facilities available to university students.

Pathways to Postgraduate Masters

If you have an Undergraduate diploma or a Higher National Diploma, and wish to progress to a Masters programme at Aston University, you may study the OnCampus Aston Graduate Diploma as a route to one of our Postgraduate programmes. 

Students who hold a Bachelors Degree, but are not eligible for direct entry on a postgraduate programme can apply for a one-year Pre-Masters and then progress to postgraduate study.

For detailed entry requirements for admission onto Pre-Masters or the Aston Graduate Diploma, please visit the OnCampus entry requirements page

For the list of postgraduate programmes, you can progress to after a Pre-Masters or the Aston Graduate Diploma programme can be found here.

Benefits of a pathway programme at ONCAMPUS Aston include the chance to:

  • Improve English language abilities
  • Develop academic and study skills, such as writing, reading and presentation
  • Understand how to be successful in a UK university
  • Develop further research study skills at master's level
  • Receive specialised university progression support


ONCAMPUS Aston takes place within Aston University's campus so students can enjoy all of the facilities available to university students.

Information on the Application Process

Application Process, Documents and Deadlines

Application Process

If you are an Aston Agent Partner, please apply via the Aston Quick Apply Portal (Postgraduate Only) - Please contact your country's main office for logins.

If you're a student applying directly, please apply via the Aston Applicant Portal (Postgraduate Only)

Undergraduate applications are to be submitted via UCAS.

Required Documents

  1. School Standard Xth and XIIth mark sheets (For UG students you may submit your predicted scores)
  2. College/University semester-wise marksheets reflecting all modules with corresponding grades
  3. University degree certificate (Provisional Degree certificates are accepted only until 2 years after graduating)
  4. Passport
  5. Personal Statement or Statement of Purpose - 600 Words Only
  6. 1 Letter of Recommendation for Postgraduate applications (excluding MBA which requires 2 Letters of Recommendation) and 1 Letter of Recommendation for Undergraduate Applications
  7. CV (if available)
  8. If you have a Master's Degree, a 200-word statement telling us why you want to study a second master level programme
  9. Any certificates or approvals needed from professional bodies (if your programme asks for it, e.g. for programmes such as MSc Mechanical Engineering, MSc OSPAP, MSc Health Psychology, etc)
  10. Any other document that the admissions team might request at the time of assessment of the submitted application

Deadlines for Application, Meeting Offer Conditions, CAS, etc can be found here.
