## Entry requirements

The following is a guide to the entry requirements for each type of degree at the University of Hull. Entry requirements may vary by course, so make sure you [check your course page](https://www.hull.ac.uk/study) for the most up to date information.

/$$

For admission onto the first year of an undergraduate bachelors degree course we generally accept the following qualifications and grades:

- Bangladeshi Higher Secondary Certificate along with a recognised foundation programme.
- International Baccalaureate Diploma with a range of 28 - 30

Please check our [undergraduate course pages](https://www.hull.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/courses) for specific entry requirements.

We also accept students who have completed a US high school curriculum.

We sometimes require subject specific grades, we will advise you of this in your offer letter.

If you do not hold one of the qualifications above, we still encourage you to submit an application. All applications will be reviewed on an individual basis.

If you wish to study with us starting from the second or final year, we are happy to consider applicants who have completed equivalent prior study. These applications are considered on a case-by-case basis.

If you have questions about these requirements or have studied a qualification or foundation programme not listed here, please email [global@hull.ac.uk](mailto:global@hull.ac.uk).

#### English language requirements

If your first language is not English, or you require a student visa to study, you will be required to provide evidence of your English language proficiency level. Find out more about our [English language requirements](https://www.hull.ac.uk/study/international-students/how-to-apply/english-language-requirements).

## Agents

You can contact one of our approved agents in your country for advice and support with your application. They can help provide guidance on our courses and entry requirements, as well as the wider University and the city. They can also assist you in submitting your application to study with us.

Our international education agents play an important role in helping us to promote our university to overseas students and we hugely value the work they do in helping students with their applications and visas and in preparing them to study in the UK. We strongly encourage our education agents to follow [The National Code of Ethical Practice for Education Agents (PDF).](https://buila-assets.ams3.cdn.digitaloceanspaces.com/docs/the_national_code_of_ethical_practice_for_uk_education_agents.pdf) The National Code sets out the standards and best practice that education agents representing UK educational institutions are expected to adhere to.
