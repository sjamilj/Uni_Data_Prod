Postgraduate Research Course

# Professional Doctorate Analytical Psychology

## Key course information

- **Course:** Analytical Psychology
- **Start date:** October 2026
- **Study mode:** Part-time
- **Duration:** 3 - 4 years
- **Location:** Colchester Campus
- **Based in:** Psychosocial and Psychoanalytic Studies

### UK entry requirements

You will need to have completed a level 8 qualification in a relevant discipline and hold membership of either BACP, BPC, UKCP, ACP or CPJA.

With your application you should provide:- Evidence of BACP, BPC, UKCP, ACP or CPJA membership
- An up to date CV
- Qualification transcript or certificate

### English language requirements

If English is not your first language, we require IELTS 7.0 overall with a minimum score of 5.5 in all components.
