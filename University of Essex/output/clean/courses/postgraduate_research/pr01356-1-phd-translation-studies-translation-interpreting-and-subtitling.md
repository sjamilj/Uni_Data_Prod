# PhD Translation Studies

## Key course information

- **Course:** Translation Studies (Translation, Interpreting and Subtitling)
- **Start date:** October 2026
- **Study mode:** Full-time
- **Duration:** 3 - 4 years
- **Location:** Colchester Campus
- **Based in:** Language, Literature, and Media (School of)

### UK entry requirements

You will need a good Masters degree,or equivalent, in translation and interpreting or a related subject such as Linguistics or Literature.  A well-developed research proposal is also essential.

You will normally be required to attend an interview/Skype interview for acceptance, and acceptance is subject to research expertise in the department.

### English language requirements

If English is not your first language we will require IELTS 7.0 overall and 6.5 in all other components.
