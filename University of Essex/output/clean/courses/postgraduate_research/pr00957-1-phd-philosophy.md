Postgraduate Research Course

# PhD Philosophy

## Key course information

- **Course:** Philosophy
- **Start date:** October 2026
- **Study mode:** Full-time
- **Duration:** 3 - 4 years
- **Location:** Colchester Campus
- **Based in:** Philosophical, Historical, and Interdisciplinary Studies (School of)

### UK entry requirements

You will need a good honours degree and a Masters degree, or equivalent, in a related subject.  A well-developed research proposal is also essential.

You will normally be required to attend an interview/Skype interview for acceptance, and acceptance is subject to research expertise in the department.

### English language requirements

If English is not your first language, we require IELTS 7.0 overall, or equivalent, with a minimum score of 5.5 in all other components.
