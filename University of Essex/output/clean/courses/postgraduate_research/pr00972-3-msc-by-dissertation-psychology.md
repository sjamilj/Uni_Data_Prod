# MSc (by Dissertation) Psychology

## Key course information

- **Course:** Psychology
- **Start date:** October 2026
- **Study mode:** Full-time
- **Duration:** 1 year - 2 years
- **Location:** Colchester Campus
- **Based in:** Psychology

### UK entry requirements

You will need a high lower second class honours degree (2:2) or equivalent in psychology or a related subject

.

You will normally be required to attend an interview/Skype interview for acceptance, and acceptance is subject to research expertise in the department.

### English language requirements

If English is not your first language , we require IELST 7.0, or equivalent with a minimum of 5.5 in all other components.
