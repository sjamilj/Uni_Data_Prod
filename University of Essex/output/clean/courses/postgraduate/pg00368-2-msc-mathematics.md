# MSc Mathematics

## Key course information

- **Course:** Mathematics
- **Start date:** October 2026
- **Study mode:** Full-time
- **Duration:** 1 year
- **Location:** Colchester Campus
- **Based in:** Mathematics, Statistics and Actuarial Science (School of)

### UK entry requirements

### A 2:2 degree or international equivalent in one of the following subjects:

- Mathematics
- Applied Mathematics
- Mathematical Statistics
- Operational Research
- Physics

We will also consider applicants with a 2:2 degree in actuarial science, computer science, or data science.

Applicants with a degree below 2:2 or equivalent will be considered dependent on any relevant professional or voluntary experience and previous modules studied. Please provide an up to date CV.

### English language requirements

If English is not your first language, we require IELTS 6.0 overall with a minimum
component score of 5.5 in all components.

If you do not meet our IELTS requirements then you may be able to complete a [pre-sessional English
pathway](https://www.essex.ac.uk/international/pre-sessional/) that enables you to start your course without retaking IELTS.
