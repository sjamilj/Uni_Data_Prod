As an international student, you must hold a relevant English Language qualification alongside your academic qualifications to apply for a [Student Route visa](https://www.canterbury.ac.uk/study-here/international/student-route-visa).

Here you will find a list of English language tests, as well as their requirements for our undergraduate and postgraduate programmes.

## English language qualifications

Run by the [British Council](https://www.britishcouncil.org/), the IELTS test is the most widely recognised and commonly available English language qualification.

### IELTS indicator/academic tests

IELTS Indicator is an online test that can be taken in-person or online. The test includes four skills: Listening, Reading, Writing, and Speaking. You will receive an indicative score for each. Your performance will be marked by IELTS examiners and you will receive your result within seven days.

| Course type | IELTS Indicator requirements (where appropriate) |
| --- | --- |
| Standard undergraduate and postgraduate programmes | 6.0 overall with no element below 5.5 |
| Professional health undergraduate programmes (including Nursing courses starting in 2026) | 7.0 overall with no element below 6.5 |
| Nursing undergraduate programmes starting 2027 onwards | 6.5 overall with no element below 6.5 |
| Non-standard postgraduate programmes | 6.5 overall with no element below 6.0 |

Taken in-person, the PTE test is designed to test international English. There are three parts to the overall test: Speaking and Writing, Reading, and Listening. You will take the online test in a test centre, and your score will be sent to you within five working days.

For further information please visit [the Pearson website](https://www.pearsonpte.com/).

| Course type | Pearson PTE score requirements (where appropriate) |
| --- | --- |
| Standard undergraduate and postgraduate programmes | 59 overall with no element below 59 |
| Professional health undergraduate programmes | 76 overall with no element below 76 |
| Non-standard postgraduate programmes | 56 overall with no element below 46 |

The TOEFL IBT can be taken either in-person or online. We cannot accept the TOEFL IBT home test.  There are four tests to complete: Reading, Listening, Speaking, and Writing.

The entire test will take around 3.5 hours to complete, including a 30 minute break. You'll receive your score within four days of completion.

For further information please visit [the TOEFL website](https://www.ets.org/toefl.html).

| Course type | TOEFL IBT score requirements (where appropriate) | TOEFL IBT SCORE requirements for tests taken from January 21st 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard undergraduate and postgraduate programmes | Minimum of 60 overall: Reading 8, Listening 7, Speaking 16 and Writing 18 | Minimum of 4 overall: Reading 4, Listening 4, Speaking 4 and Writing 4. |
| Professional health undergraduate programmes | Minimum of 94 overall: Reading 19, Listening 20, Speaking 20, and Writing 24 | Minimum of 5 overall: Reading 4.5, Listening 4.5, Speaking 4.5, and Writing 4.5 |
| Non-standard postgraduate programmes | Minimum of 79 overall: Reading 13, Listening 12, Speaking 18 and Writing 21 | Minimum of 5 overall: Reading 4.5, Listening 4.5, Speaking 4.5, and Writing 4.5 |
