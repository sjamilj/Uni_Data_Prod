### The CCCU International Student Scholarship

- offers £1,500 off the standard full-time international tuition fee of £15,000 (for 2026/27 entry) per year. This amounts to £4,500 off across a three-year degree.

If you meet these conditions, the scholarship is automatically awarded.


## How do you apply for an international scholarship?

You can find out all the details and criteria about each scholarship on the relevant page, but in summary:

- The International Student Scholarship is automatically applied to all international students who meet the criteria.
