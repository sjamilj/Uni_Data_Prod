##### Financial Support and Scholarships for International Students

At Buckinghamshire New University, we believe in the power of education to transform lives. We are dedicated to providing high-quality education and creating opportunities for students to excel academically and personally. We recognise the financial challenges international students face when moving abroad and we are committed to providing various avenues of support to ensure that your academic journey is both enriching and accessible. Through a range of financial support and scholarships, we aim to make quality education affordable for international students seeking to pursue their academic goals at BNU.

BNU Refugee Scholarship

Buckinghamshire New University is committed to supporting refugees and providing them with opportunities to make the most of their new lives.

Eligibility Criteria

- Provide Evidence of your Refugee status in accordance with government guidance
- Be ineligible to access funding from the Student Loan
- You have a conditional or unconditional offer from Buckinghamshire New University
- You must be getting enrolled onto a full-time undergraduate, postgraduate or foundation course in one of our High Wycombe or Uxbridge campuses
- Not already hold a qualification at the same level you are applying for.

Guidance on obtaining a Share Code and can be found [here](https://www.gov.uk/view-prove-immigration-status).

The scholarship will cover:

- The cost of all course tuition fees
- A Maintenance allowance of �2600 each year of your course for the duration of your studies
- Accommodation for the duration of the course of study in University Halls of Residence*
- Free gym membership
- Access to BNU New Usage scheme

(*students must be in our University Halls of Residence to receive this. Students living in the private sector will not be able to receive this)

Further Information

- There is only a very limited amount of scholarship spaces available and completing this application does not guarantee funding for your course which is a separate application
- This scholarship is only able to assist an individual and cannot provide support for family members or friends
- Students must inform the bursary team of any changes to their immigration status during their studies at Bursary@bnu.ac.uk .

How to apply

We have a limited number of spaces on this scholarship and applications have now closed as of 24/07/2026 for students starting on the September intake for the 26/27 academic year.

BNU Asylum Seeker Scholarship

Buckinghamshire New University is committed to supporting Asylum Seekers and providing them with opportunities to make the most of their new lives.

- You have a conditional or unconditional offer from Buckinghamshire New University
- You must be getting enrolled onto a full-time undergraduate, postgraduate or foundation course in one of our High Wycombe or Uxbridge campuses
- Not already hold a qualification at the same level you are applying for.

More information on Application Registration Cards (ARC) can be found [here](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/application-registration-card-arc/application-registration-card-arc).
