Undergraduate entry

As part of our ongoing commitment to visa compliance, we are currently unable to consider undergraduate applications from students whose most recent studies were completed in Bangladesh.

Year of Entry: MastersMinimum Entry Qualifications- Bachelor's honours degree (minimum 4 years in duration) with GPA 2.7 or 55%.
- **University must be listed on the University Grants Commission of Bangladesh list -** http://www.ugc-universities.gov.bd/
- Bachelor of Architecture (when studied for at least 5 years, and either awarded by a public university or awarded from 2014 onwards by a private university accredited by the Institute of Architects (IAB)).
- Bachelor of Dental Surgery (When studied at an institution listed on ECCTIS for this qualification and studied at a dental college recognised by the BM&DC. 4 year duration up to and including 2016, 5 year duration 2017 onwards.
- Bachelor of Medicine and Surgery (When studied at one of the institutions listed on ECCTIS for this qualification) 5 year duration.
- Bachelor of Science in Engineering.
- Masters Degree when following a two year ‘Bachelor Pass’ or three year Bachelor Honours degree (up to 2007) 50%.
- Masters Degree when following a three year ‘Bachelor Pass’ or four year Bachelor degree) 50%.
- 2:1 equivalent percentage: 60% OR 2:2 equivalent percentage: 55%.
Minimum English Language requirements

IELTS 6.5 with no element under 6.0 or equivalent.
