Foundation Year courses - September start

Whether you have non-traditional qualifications, want to change your subjects, have had a break from education and wish to return or want a change of career – the foundation years at Keele provide an opportunity for those with diverse backgrounds to access an honours degree. Upon successful completion, you will progress onto your degree programme in September.

The courses below are for September 2027.

You can also view our list of Foundation Year courses that start in January. Please note, our January entry for Foundation Year offers a condensed programme (January-September).
