# English language requirements

## English language entry requirements 2027/28

All students applying to Keele must show they can meet our minimum English language requirements, in line with the English Language Group listed on the relevant course page.

Please note:the English Language [test group bands](https://www.keele.ac.uk/study/undergraduate/apply/entryrequirements/internationalentryrequirements/englishlanguagerequirements/#english-language-tests-(groups)) listed on this page relate to Undergraduate and Postgraduate courses.

#### Information on this page relates to the 2027/28 academic year.

For the 2026/27 academic year, please see the 2026 English Language web pages.

For most UK students, English language requirements will be met with a 4 or C in GCSE English. Meeting our minimum English requirements will also typically meet the English requirements for students who require a visa to study in the UK.

All postgraduate students will also need to meet our minimum English language requirements if they require a visa. Please see the relevant course page.

Our requirements can be met in any of the following ways:

- An English language qualification;
- An English language test;
- A specific exemption.

### 2027/28 Entry

The information below is for 2027/28 entry (September 2027 or January 2028) and is updated on an annual basis. It does not apply for A100 Medicine or D100 Veterinary Science and their associated foundation year courses, or for MSc Physician Associate Studies. For these, the relevant course pages outline how these entry requirements can be met. It also does not apply to our International Foundation Year, Year One, or Pre-Master’s courses delivered through our International College, our online-only Master’s courses, study abroad programmes, or any apprenticeships, short courses, or CPD.

For all students requiring a visa, we will consider your most recent English language qualification as the current indication of your level of English. If you believe you do not meet the entry requirements stated and hold international fee status or require a visa, please read the information below on pre-sessional courses.

### Exemptions

For postgraduate students only, you do not need to provide an English qualification if you’re from one of the following countries or territories, or you’ve completed a qualification equivalent to a UK Bachelors degree in one of them in the last two years:

- Antigua and Barbuda
- Australia
- the Bahamas
- Barbados
- Belize
- the British overseas territories
- Dominica
- Grenada
- Guyana
- Ireland
- Jamaica
- Malta
- New Zealand
- St Kitts and Nevis
- St Lucia
- St Vincent and the Grenadines
- Trinidad and Tobago
- UK
- USA

You also do not need to prove your knowledge of English if the following applies:

- you’re a national of Canada

## What if you don’t have the required level of English?

##### Pre-Sessional English

If you would like to study at Keele University and need help achieving our English language requirements, our pre-sessional course may help you meet the language conditions of your offer. [Find out more.](https://www.keele.ac.uk/study/languagecentre/pre-sessional-courseoverview/)

##### Keele University International Foundation Year

Our International Foundation Year is delivered on campus through our Keele University International College (KUIC).

Find out more about the International Foundation Year options on the [KUIC website](https://kuic.keele.ac.uk/).
