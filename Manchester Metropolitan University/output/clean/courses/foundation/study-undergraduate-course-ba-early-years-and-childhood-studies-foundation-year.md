# BA (Hons) Early Years and Childhood Studies (Foundation Year)

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 80
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time
- **UCAS code(s):** X341

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £20,000 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

#### IELTS

IELTS 5.5 with no component below 5.5 taken within two years of course start date. If you plan to meet the Level 2 course requirements through your IB Diploma you will need to achieve Higher Level 4 or Standard Level 5 in English

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
