# Foundation Year Science and Engineering degrees with a foundation year (Environmental Science route)

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 72-80
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £21,500 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

[ [

#### IB Diploma

IB Diploma - Pass overall with a minimum overall score of 24 or minimum 72 UCAS Tariff points from three Higher Level subjects. ]

#### IELTS

IELTS 5.5 with no component below 5.5, taken within two years of course start date

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)
