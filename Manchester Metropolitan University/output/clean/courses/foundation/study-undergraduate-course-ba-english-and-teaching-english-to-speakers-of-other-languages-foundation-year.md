# BA (Hons) English And Teaching English To Speakers Of Other Languages (Foundation Year)

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 72-80
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time
- **UCAS code(s):** TEN0 (with foundation year)

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £20,000 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

[ [

#### IB Diploma

IB Diploma with minimum 24 points overall or 72 UCAS Tariff points from three Higher Level subjects. If you plan to meet the Level 2 course requirements through your IB Diploma you will need to achieve Higher Level 4 or Standard Level 5 in English ]

#### IELTS

5.5 overall with no component below 5.5, taken within two years of course start date

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)
