# MSc Advanced Physiotherapy

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

A physiotherapy UK honours degree (or international equivalent) or graduate diploma in physiotherapy with evidence of approved and relevant post-qualification education is usual.

Applicants whose first language is not English must provide evidence of proficiency in the English language. The minimum level of proficiency for acceptance on to this programme is an IELTS overall score of 6.5 or equivalent, with no area less than 5.5.

Application:

The quickest and most efficient way to apply for this course is to apply online. This way, you can also track your application at each stage of the process.

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)
