# MA History

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

A 2.1 degree or higher in History or another humanities discipline. Those who have a master’s from a non-Humanities field will be considered on the merits of their individual application, and might be asked to submit, for example, a piece of written work. Those without a humanities degree or equivalent qualification will also be considered if they have compensating industry experience.

English Language Requirements
IELTS 6.5
TOEFL 575
TOEFL computer-based 230

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.

### Alternative entry route

If you do not meet the entry requirements for this programme, you may still be eligible to join Manchester Metropolitan University International College through a [pre-masters](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international/college/study/pre-masters/humanities-social-science) pathway. This programme is designed to help you build the academic skills and subject knowledge needed to progress to your chosen Master's at Manchester Metropolitan University.
