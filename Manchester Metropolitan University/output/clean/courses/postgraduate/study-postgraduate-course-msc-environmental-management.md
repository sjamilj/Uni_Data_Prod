# MSc Environmental Management

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £22,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time; 2 years full-time with placement

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £22,000 per year

## Entry requirements

An undergraduate degree of at least 2:2 Honours or equivalent. Exceptions may be possible for applicants who can demonstrate highly relevant work experience in industry over several years. A passion for environmental issues and sustainable development is key. Each application is considered on its own merit.

English requirements: IELTS 6.5, with no component below 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification.

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
