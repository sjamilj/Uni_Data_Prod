# MA Social Research

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** Full-time: one year

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You’ll need to have a 2:2 UK Honours degree, or an alternative postgraduate or professional qualification recognised as being equivalent to a 2:2 UK Honours degree. Other qualifications or experience may be acceptable if you are able to demonstrate appropriate knowledge and skills to a UK Honours degree standard.

Eligible applicants will be invited to attend an online or in-person interview with the programme lead (or their nominated representative) as part of the admissions process.

Language requirements

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification. Accepted English qualifications can be viewed [here](https://www2.mmu.ac.uk/international/courses/english-language-requirements/).

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
