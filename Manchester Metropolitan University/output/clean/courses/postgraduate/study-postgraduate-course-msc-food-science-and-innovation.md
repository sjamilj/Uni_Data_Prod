# MSc Food Science and Innovation

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** Full-time: 1 year

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

Typically, you will have a minimum 2:2 undergraduate honours degree, or an international equivalent, in a natural or applied science discipline or in an engineering discipline, including Food Science and Technology, Food engineering, Biotechnology, Biochemistry, Nutrition, Chemical engineering, agricultural sciences, Biomedical sciences, biology or related subject. Good honours candidates from other subjects may still be eligible if they can demonstrate significant proven professional experience in a relevant field.

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 6.0 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification. Accepted English qualifications can be viewed [here](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements).

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
