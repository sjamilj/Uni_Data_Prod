# MSc Construction Project Management

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You’ll need a UK honours degree – at least a 2:2 – or the international equivalent in Engineering, Construction, Management, Business, Humanities, or equivalent. Applicants with relevant and extensive professional experience in the relevant fields will be considered.

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification.  Completion of the our [Pre-Masters in Engineering and Computing](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international/college/study/pre-masters/engineering-computing#ai-116007-0) is also an accepted entry route to the course.

International students please see [mmu.ac.uk/international](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international)

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
