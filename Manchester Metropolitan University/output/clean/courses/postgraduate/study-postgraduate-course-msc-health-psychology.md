# MSc Health Psychology

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

A 2:2 or above in a BPS-accredited psychology undergraduate degree (or equivalent through a psychology conversion course) conferring Graduate Basis for Chartered Membership (GBC) is required.

International applicants have to show evidence that their undergraduate degree is eligible for GBC. Applicants must contact the British Psychological Society independently to verify this and upload the proof along with their application. For information, please check the[BPS website.](https://www.bps.org.uk/join-us/membership/graduate-membership)

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification. Accepted English qualifications can be viewed [here](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements).

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
