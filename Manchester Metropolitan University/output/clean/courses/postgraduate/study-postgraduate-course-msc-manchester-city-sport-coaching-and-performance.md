# MSc Manchester City Sport Coaching and Performance

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £22,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £22,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You’ll need at least a 2:2 undergraduate degree or equivalent in a related area, including, but not limited to, Sport Coaching, Sport Development, Sport and Exercise Sciences, Data Analytics, or Health Sciences; or in an area of study accompanied by professional or personal experience. We may consider your application for Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) and Accredited Prior Experience (APE) entrance if you have relevant, extensive professional experience in this field, even if you don’t have the required qualifications.

IELTS requirements for international students are set at 6.5 with no element below 5.5. Further information for international students is available for applicants with non-UK qualifications is available at [mmu.ac.uk/international](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international).

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
