# MSc Sport and Exercise Science

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You’ll need a UK honours degree – at least a 2:2 undergraduate degree or equivalent in a related area, including but not limited to, Sport and Exercise Sciences, Sport Coaching, Human Biology, Nutritional Sciences, Health Sciences, Physiology, Psychology, or Human Movement; or in an area of study accompanied by professional or personal experience. Applicants with relevant extensive professional experience in this field will be considered for Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) and Accredited Prior Experience (APE) entrance, even if they do not have the required qualifications.

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification.

Further information for international students is available for applicants with non-UK qualifications is available at [mmu.ac.uk/international](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international)
