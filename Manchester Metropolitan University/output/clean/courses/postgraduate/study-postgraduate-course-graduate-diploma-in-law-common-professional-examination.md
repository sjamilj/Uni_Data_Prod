# Graduate Diploma in Law (Common Professional Examination)

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £12,900
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £12,900 per year

## Entry requirements

You should have an honours degree, at least a 2:2, from the UK or the Republic of Ireland. We may also consider your application if you do not have a degree, but have appropriate professional experience.

If you have any questions about our entry requirements, please [contact our Course Enquiries Team](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/contact/course-enquiry/).

International students with a degree are eligible to apply as long as equivalence to a UK/ROI honours degree can be demonstrated.  You must be able to show that you have a good command of both written and spoken English. We typically require an IELTS score of 7.5 or equivalent.

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
