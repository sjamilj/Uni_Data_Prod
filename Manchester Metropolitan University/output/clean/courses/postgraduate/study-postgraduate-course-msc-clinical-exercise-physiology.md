# MSc Clinical Exercise Physiology

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Upper second-class UK honours degree (2:1)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

At least a 2:1 undergraduate degree or equivalent in a relevant science or health-related subject (e.g. Sport and Exercise Science or Human Physiology).

IELTS requirements for international students are set at an overall score of 6.5 with no element below 6.0 for reading and writing and 6.0 for speaking and listening.

Further information for international students is available for applicants with non-UK qualifications is available at [mmu.ac.uk/international](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international)

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
