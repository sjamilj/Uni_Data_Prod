# MSc Child and Adolescent Mental Health

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Upper second-class UK honours degree (2:1)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

Undergraduate 2:1 award or above (or overseas equivalent) in Psychology or related to the allied health/social care professions e.g. psychology, medicine, nursing, occupational therapy, social work, speech and language therapy, physiotherapy, education is typically required.

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification. Accepted English qualifications can be viewed here.

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
