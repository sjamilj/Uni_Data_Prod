# MSc Human Nutrition

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You will have a minimum 2:2 UK undergraduate degree, or equivalent, in a relevant science or health-based subject. Relevant subject areas include biological and environmental sciences, physiology, biochemistry, chemistry, geography, public health, health and social care, social work, nursing, and psychology. Students with a degree outside of these subject areas will be considered if they can demonstrate a sound underpinning knowledge in a science or health-related subject.

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 6.0 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification. Accepted English qualifications can be viewed [here](https://www2.mmu.ac.uk/international/courses/english-language-requirements/).

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
