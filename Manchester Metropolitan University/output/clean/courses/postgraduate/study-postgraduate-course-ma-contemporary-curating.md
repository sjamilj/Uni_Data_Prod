# MA Contemporary Curating

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £22,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £22,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You will normally have an undergraduate UK honours degree, or international equivalent or equivalent UK qualification from an arts or humanities background in a subject that includes some coverage of art history, fine art, or cultural studies at honours degree standard. Alternatively, if your undergraduate degree is not arts or humanities-based you should be able to adequately demonstrate its pertinence or articulate its relevance in your personal statement and how it informs your aspiration to study masters programme in the field of curating.

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification. Accepted English qualifications can be viewed [here](https://www2.mmu.ac.uk/international/courses/english-language-requirements/).

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.

### Alternative entry route

If you do not meet the entry requirements for this programme, you may still be eligible to join Manchester Metropolitan University International College through a [pre-masters](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international/college/study/pre-masters/humanities-social-science) pathway. This programme is designed to help you build the academic skills and subject knowledge needed to progress to your chosen Master's at Manchester Metropolitan University.
