# MSc Project Management

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You’ll need a UK honours degree in any subject, at least a 2:2, or the international equivalent. We’ll also welcome an application if you don’t have a degree, but you have the equivalent professional qualification or significant relevant management experience.

English Language Requirements

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 6.5 with no less than 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification.

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.

### Country-specific entry requirements

[Find your country](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/international-students) for specific entry requirements

### Alternative entry route

If you do not meet the entry requirements for this programme, you may still be eligible to join Manchester Metropolitan University International College through a [pre-masters](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international/college/study/pre-masters/business-management-finance) pathway. This programme is designed to help you build the academic skills and subject knowledge needed to progress to your chosen Master's at Manchester Metropolitan University.
