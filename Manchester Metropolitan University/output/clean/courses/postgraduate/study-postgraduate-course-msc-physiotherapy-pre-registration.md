# MSc Physiotherapy (Pre-registration)

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £22,000
- **Course length:** Full-time: 2 years
- **UCAS code(s):** MSC1

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £22,000 per year

## Entry requirements

An undergraduate honours degree (upper second class or above) in a relevant subject or the equivalent qualification. Relevant subject areas include: biological sciences, sports science, sports therapy or rehabilitation, chemistry, nursing and psychology.

GCSE Mathematics grade C/4 or above.

Please note if English is not your first language, we also require one of the following qualifications:

IELTS: An overall mark of 7 with not less than 6.5 in any one component.

TOEFL: 600 points paper based.

Additional requirements:

- Evidence of interest and work experience in the relevant field.
- Evidence of studying within the last five years.
- A satisfactory Enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check is required for this programme. Applicants who have lived outside the UK must provide evidence of a satisfactory overseas police check.

The 'Personal Statement' section of the application form is for you to provide additional information to support your application. Personal Statement guidance can be found via the link in the 'How to apply for this course' section.
