# MBA Master of Business Administration

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2) and 3 years experience

## Entry requirements

Entry requirements:

This course is open to candidates who have leadership or management experience and are ready to take their career to the next level.

To be considered you’ll need:

Academic criteria

- A minimum of a lower-second class honours (2:2) bachelor’s degree, or an international equivalent qualification.

Professional Experience Criteria

- A minimum of 3 years post-graduation work experience at a professional or managerial level

If you would like us to review your CV ahead of applying, please email a copy of your CV or a link to your LinkedIn profile to [mbaenquiries@mmu.ac.uk](mailto:mbaenquiries@mmu.ac.uk)

Admissions Personal Statement Criteria

Manchester Met MBA Candidates are asked to provide a personal statement with their application – this is your opportunity to share more about your experience and motivations.

In the region of 450-500 words, you should consider:

- What has motivated you to pursue an MBA?
- Your post-MBA career objectives
- Why you feel the Manchester Met MBA is the right fit for you
- How your professional experience to-date has prepared you for MBA study
- What you will bring to the MBA cohort

Professional entry route

Candidates who do not hold a degree-level qualification but who can demonstrate extensive professional experience (typically in the region of 10 years) are invited to discuss their background with our team: mbaenquiries@mmu.ac.uk

International applicants

As a part-time programme, this course is not eligible for student visa sponsorship, however you will be required to attend workshop sessions in Manchester, which many students can do with a standard visitor visa. You can find out more about the visa requirements for your country, here: [https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa](https://www.gov.uk/check-uk-visa) or you can email [mba.admissions@mmu.ac.uk](mailto:mba.admissions@mba.ac.uk)

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
