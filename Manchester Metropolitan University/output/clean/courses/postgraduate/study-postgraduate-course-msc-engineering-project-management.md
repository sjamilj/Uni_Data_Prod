# MSc Engineering Project Management

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class UK honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £22,000
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time 2 years full-time with placement (September intake only)

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £22,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You'll need a UK honours degree – at least a 2:2 – or the international equivalent, in engineering, physics, mathematics, computing or equivalent. Applicants with relevant and extensive professional experience in the relevant fields will be considered.

International studentsplease see [mmu.ac.uk/study/international.](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international)

English requirements: IELTS 6.5, with no component below 5.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification.

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.

[Find your country](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/international-students) for specific entry requirements

### Alternative entry route

If you do not meet the entry requirements for this programme, you may still be eligible to join Manchester Metropolitan University International College through a [pre-masters](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/international/college/study/pre-masters/engineering-computing) pathway. This programme is designed to help you build the academic skills and subject knowledge needed to progress to your chosen Master's at Manchester Metropolitan University.
