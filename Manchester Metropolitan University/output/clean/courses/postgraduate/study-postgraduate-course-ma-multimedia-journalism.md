# MA Multimedia Journalism

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** Lower second-class honours degree (2:2)
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £22,000

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £22,000 per year

## Entry requirements

You will normally have a lower second class UK honours degree (or international equivalent) in any discipline.

Applicants whose first language is not English (and whose first degree was not taught in English) are required to produce evidence of English Language proficiency.

Overseas applicants will require IELTS with an overall score of 7.0 with no less than 6.5 in any category, or an equivalent accepted English qualification. Accepted English qualifications can be viewed [here](https://www2.mmu.ac.uk/international/courses/english-language-requirements/).

If you have any questions about experience or entry requirements for the course, you are encouraged to contact the course leader Andrew Dickinson [A.Dickinson@mmu.ac.uk](mailto:A.Dickinson@mmu.ac.uk).

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
