# BA (Hons) Games Art

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 104-112
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,500
- **Course length:** 3 years full-time; 4 years full-time with placement; 4 years full-time with Foundation
- **UCAS code(s):** WGA1

## Fees and funding

International full-time fee £21,500 per year
