# MEng (Hons) Electrical and Electronic Engineering

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 112-120
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,500
- **Course length:** 4 years full-time; 5 years with placement
- **UCAS code(s):** 2W69

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,500 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

[ [

IB Diploma - Pass overall with a minimum overall score of 28 or minimum 112 UCAS Tariff points from three Higher Level subjects, to include HL Mathematics: Analysis and Approaches ]

6.0 with no component below 5.5, taken within two years of course start date
