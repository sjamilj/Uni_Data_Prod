# BA (Hons) Events Management

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 104-112
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £20,000
- **Course length:** 3 years full-time; 4 years with placement; 4 years overseas study; 4 years with foundation
- **UCAS code(s):** N822 (with foundation year); N821

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £20,000 per year
International full-time fee £20,000 per year
