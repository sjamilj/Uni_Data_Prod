# BSc (Hons) Sport and Exercise Nutrition

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 104-112
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,500
- **Course length:** 3 years full-time, 4 years with placement
- **UCAS code(s):** S2N5 (with foundation year); S2N6

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £20,000 per year
International full-time fee £21,500 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

[ [

#### IB Diploma

IB Diploma - Pass overall with a minimum overall score of 26 or minimum 104 UCAS Tariff points from three Higher Level subjects, to include at least one of Biology, Chemistry, Physics or Sports, Exercise and Health Science. ]

#### IELTS

6.5 with no component below 6.0, taken within two years of course start date
