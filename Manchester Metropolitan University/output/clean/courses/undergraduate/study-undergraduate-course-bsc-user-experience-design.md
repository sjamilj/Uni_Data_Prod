# BSc (Hons) User Experience Design

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 104-112
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,500
- **Course length:** 3 years full-time; 4 years full-time with placement year; 4 years full-time with study abroad year; 4 years full-time with foundation year

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £21,500 per year
International full-time fee £21,500 per year
