# BA (Hons) Business Management and French

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 104-112
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £20,000
- **Course length:** 4 years full-time, with study abroad

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £20,000 per year
International full-time fee £20,000 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

[ [

#### IB Diploma

IB Diploma with minimum 26 points overall or 104 UCAS tariff points from three Higher Level subjects including H5 in French. If you plan to meet the Level 2 course requirements through your IB Diploma you will need to achieve Higher Level 4 or Standard Level 5 in English and Higher Level 4 or Standard Level 4 in Mathematics ]

#### IELTS

6.0 overall with no component below 5.5, taken within two years of course start date

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)
