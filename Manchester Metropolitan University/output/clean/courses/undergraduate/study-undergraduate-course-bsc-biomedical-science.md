# BSc (Hons) Biomedical Science

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 112-120
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,500
- **Course length:** 4 years with a placement; 4 years with foundation year
- **UCAS code(s):** C742 (with foundation year); C740

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £21,500 per year
International full-time fee £21,500 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

[ [

#### IB Diploma

Pass the IB Diploma with minimum 28 points overall or 112 UCAS Tariff points from three Higher Level subjects, including HL5 in Biology. If you plan to meet the Level 2 course requirements through your IB Diploma you will need to achieve Higher Level 4 or Standard Level 5 in English and Higher Level 4 or Standard Level 4 in Mathematics ]

#### IELTS

6.0 overall with no component below 5.5, taken within two years of course start date
