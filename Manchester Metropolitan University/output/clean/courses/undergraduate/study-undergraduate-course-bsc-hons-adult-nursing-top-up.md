# BSc (Hons) Adult Nursing (Top Up)

## Fact file

- **Start date:** March 2027
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £5375 per 30 credits
- **Course length:** 18 months full-time
- **UCAS code(s):** B777

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £5375 per 30 credits

## Entry requirements

### International students

#### IELTS

This course is designed for registered Nursing Associates only. IELTS 6.5 overall with a minimum score of 6.5 in Reading, Listening and Speaking and 6.0 in Writing, taken within two years of the course start date.

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
