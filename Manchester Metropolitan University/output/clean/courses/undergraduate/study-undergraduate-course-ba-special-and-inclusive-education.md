# BA (Hons) Special and Inclusive Education

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 104-112
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £20,000
- **Course length:** 3 years full-time
- **UCAS code(s):** SIE1

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £20,000 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

[ [

#### IB Diploma

28 IB Diploma Points to include HL 4 or SL 5 in English and Mathematics. ]

#### IELTS

IELTS 6.0 with no component below 5.5, taken within two years of the start of this course.
