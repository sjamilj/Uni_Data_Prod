# BSc (Hons) Sport Coaching and Development

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 112-120
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,500
- **Course length:** 3 years full-time; 4 years with placement; 4 years with foundation year
- **UCAS code(s):** C622 (with foundation year); C621

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £21,500 per year
International full-time fee £21,500 per year
