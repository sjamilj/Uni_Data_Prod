# BA (Hons) International Interior Design (Top Up)

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £21,500
- **Course length:** 1 year full-time
- **UCAS code(s):** IID6

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International full-time fee £21,500 per year

## Entry requirements

### International students

#### IELTS

International students will be required to achieve an IELTS score of 6.0 with no component less than 5.5, or an equivalent accepted English language qualification. This is in line with the University’s entry requirements for students whose first language is not English.

[Find out about English language requirements](/study/international/before-you-apply/english-language-requirements)

If you are an international student, you will usually need to [get a student visa](https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-your-visa) before you can study in the UK.
