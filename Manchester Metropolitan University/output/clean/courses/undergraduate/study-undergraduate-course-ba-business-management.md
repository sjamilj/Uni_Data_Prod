# BA (Hons) Business Management

## Fact file

- **Start date:** September 2026
- **Typical offer:** 120-128
- **Typical annual fees:** Overseas £20,000
- **Course length:** 3 years full-time 4 years placement 4 years overseas study
- **UCAS code(s):** N203 (with foundation year); N201

## Fees and funding

### EU and non-EU international students

International foundation fee £20,000 per year
International full-time fee £20,000 per year
