Apply for your CAS

The first step towards getting your visa to study in the UK.

Apply for your CAS

Your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS)

A Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS) is a reference number Manchester Met gives you. You need it to apply for your Student visa.

Your CAS confirms that:

  • you have an unconditional offer to study with us;
  • which course you are studying and for how long; and
  • that we are sponsoring your Student visa application.

Each CAS can only be used once, for one visa application. If you need to apply for a visa again later, for example if you need more time to finish your course or your visa application is refused, you’ll need a new CAS.

If you’re studying at our International College, your visa process is different. See International College visa guidance.

Before you can get your CAS

You’ll need to do three things first:

  • Meet all the conditions of your offer, for example sending final transcripts.
  • Accept your unconditional offer.
  • Pay your tuition fee deposit, or send us evidence that you don’t need to pay one.

You should also read the CAS policy for new students before you apply.

Pay your deposit

Most students pay a £5,000 deposit. Check how to pay your deposit.

Pay your deposit as early as you can. A late deposit is the most common reason CAS requests are delayed.

How we issue your CAS

Once you’ve met the conditions above, we’ll email a secure CAS Shield link to the personal email address on your application, from mmu@cas-shield.com. Check your junk or spam folder if you don’t see it.

As part of this, some applicants are asked to complete a short, pre-recorded interview through CAS Shield. You may need to show you’re a credible student and that you can afford to study before we can issue a CAS. If it applies to you, we’ll email you instructions directly, you don’t need to request this yourself.

This is separate from any interview UKVI itself may ask you to attend as part of your actual visa application. If that happens, UKVI will contact you directly and tell you what to expect.

Key dates for September 2026 entry

These dates are for applicants making visa applications overseas and for most courses starting on 28 September 2026

If your course starts before 28 September 2026, earlier deadlines may apply. Contact IntlPreEnrolment@mmu.ac.uk if you are unsure when you need to request your CAS by.

Differentiation arrangements

The Home Office publishes a fixed list of nationalities that qualify for reduced evidence requirements — sometimes called “differentiation arrangements” or the “differential evidence requirement.” 

Check the current list on GOV.UK (Appendix Student, paragraph 22.1) if you’re not sure which deadline applies to you.

  • Applying from outside the UK

    Applying from outside the UK

    The earliest we can issue your CAS is 6 months before your course starts.

    If you’re not from a nationality on UKVI’s differentiation arrangements list, you’ll need to show evidence that you meet UKVI’s financial requirements as part of your visa application, and we may ask to see this before we issue your CAS. See what financial evidence you’ll need.

    Some applicants are also asked to show they’re a credible student before we issue a CAS — this is one of our own checks, separate from anything UKVI may ask of you directly.

  • Applying from inside the UK

    Applying from inside the UK

    The earliest we can issue your CAS is 3 months before your course starts.

    If you’re extending or changing your course, you’ll usually need to show you meet the academic progression requirement, broadly, that your new course is at a higher level than your previous one, or is closely related to it and that you have successfully completed your previous course of study.

    • Read UKCISA’s guidance on applying for a Student visa in the UK.
  • Extending your stay on your current course

    Extending your stay on your current course

    If you’re staying on your current course and need a new CAS, use one of these forms instead of the standard process:

    • Extending your visa to continue your current course
    • Returning from a suspension of study

    Read the CAS policy for enrolled students before you apply.

  • If your request for a CAS is refused – Appeals

    If your request for a CAS is refused – Appeals

    1. Appeals submitted under our CAS policy fall under two strands: 
       

    a. Applicants and visiting or exchange students requesting CAS for a new course of study; and   
    b. Students extending their time on an existing programme of study 
     

    1. Applicants appealing under 1(a) must submit an appeal request within five working days of their appeal decision and no less than 5 weeks before the latest enrolment date for their course via an online form: https://forms.office.com/e/8nEqqPK91
    2. Any additional evidence must then be submitted to compliancemanager@mmu.ac.uk
    3. Appeals outside of the window of 1(a) may still be considered, but not necessarily for the intake the student was intending to apply for due to visa decision times.
    4. Appeals will be considered under 1(a) by a 3-person panel chaired by the Director of Global Engagement or a delegate.
    5. Appeals under 1(b) should be submitted via the normal channels for student complaints and appeals: https://www.mmu.ac.uk/student-life/student-case-management/guidance-for-students/student-complaints-procedure

Frequently asked questions

  • What if I miss a deadline?

    What if I miss a deadline?

    Contact us as soon as possible. We may not be able to issue your CAS in time for your original course start date, so it’s important to tell us early.

  • What if I'm asked to do an online interview?

    What if I'm asked to do an online interview?

    Not everyone is asked to do this. If you are, we’ll email you a link and instructions. You don’t need to request it yourself, and there’s nothing to prepare beyond answering honestly about your course, your finances, your reasons for studying at Manchester Met and overall career plans. 

    Please see: https://www.mmu.ac.uk/study/international/when-you-have-an-offer/secure-your-place/apply-for-cas/show-you-are-a-credible-student

  • Can I get more than one CAS?

    Can I get more than one CAS?

    Each CAS can only be used for one visa application. If your first application is refused, or you need to apply again for another reason, contact us to discuss a new CAS. 

    You can contact us via CAS Shield if your visa application is refused.

  • What happens if my visa application is refused?

    What happens if my visa application is refused?

    Contact us straight away via your CAS Shield account, we’ll explain what a refusal means for your place at Manchester Met and what your options are.

  • I'm studying at the International College — does this apply to me?

    I'm studying at the International College — does this apply to me?

    No. The International College manages its own visa processes. See International College visa guidance.
