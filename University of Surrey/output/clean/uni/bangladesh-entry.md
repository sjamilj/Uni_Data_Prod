# Entry requirements

See our country-specific equivalencies.

## Undergraduate

Please contact [admissions@surrey.ac.uk](mailto:admissions@surrey.ac.uk) for enquiries about applying using the Bangladeshi High School Certificate.

### Undergraduate pathway programmes

If you do not meet the direct entry requirements for a course, you can apply to study an [International Foundation Year](https://isc.surrey.ac.uk/programmes/international-foundation-year) programme at the [University of Surrey International Study Centre](https://isc.surrey.ac.uk/). Upon successful completion, you can progress to an undergraduate degree at the University of Surrey.

## Postgraduate

You can apply for our postgraduate masters courses if you have completed a four-year degree from a recognised Bangladesh university.

For courses that require a UK 2:1, you must achieve a GPA of at least 3.25 out of 4.0, 3.5 out of 4.0, or 3.75 out of 4.0, depending on the institution at which you studied.

For courses that require a UK 2:2, you must achieve a GPA of at least 3.0 out of 4.0, 3.25 out of 4.0, or 3.5 out of 4.0, depending on the institution at which you studied.

### Postgraduate taught pathway programmes

If you do not meet the requirements for a postgraduate course, you can apply for a [Pre-Masters Preparation Programme](https://isc.surrey.ac.uk/programmes/pre-masters-preparation-programme). These pathway programmes are offered by the [University of Surrey International Study Centre](https://isc.surrey.ac.uk/) and are designed in order to develop a student’s academic and English language skills in preparation for postgraduate study.

Upon successful completion, you can progress to a masters degree at the University of Surrey.

## English language requirements and courses

All our students require a good standard of English language to be eligible to study with us. If you don’t meet our requirements, we offer intensive pre-sessional English language courses, designed to take you to the level of English ability and skill required for your studies.
