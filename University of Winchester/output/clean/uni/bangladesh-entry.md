# Entry Requirements For International Students

Our International students come from all over the world and we understand that something’s are a little different when applying and then arriving at the University. We have therefore provided a list of some of the countries and regions we work in with specific information included on Entry Requirements, Funding Opportunities, Visas and other useful information. If your home country or region is not listed or if you have a question about something that is not included then please contact us.

Find out more about our [English language entry requirements](https://www.winchester.ac.uk/international/english-language-requirements/).

## Your Country/Region (A-Z)

### Argentina

The University of Winchester welcomes students from Argentina onto a range of courses.  All information below relates specifically to Argentinian students.

### Foundation entry qualifications

Students applying for a Foundation programme at Winchester will be required hold the following qualification :-

- Holders of the Titulo de Bachillerato (BACH) with a minimum of 5/10 will be considered for admission to a Foundation programme

### Undergraduate entry qualifications

For language requirements, please visit our [English Language Requirements page](https://www.winchester.ac.uk/international/english-language-requirements/).

All students applying for a Bachelors (BA, BSc and LLB) degree at Winchester will be also be required hold one of the following qualifications:

- Successful completion of a suitable one year UK Foundation programme from a recognised university or further education college
- Holders of the European Baccalaureate with a minimum 6.5/65% depending on the subject chosen
- Holders of the International Baccalaureate Diploma " a score of at least 24-26 is usually required
- Successful completion of one year a recognised university in Argentina

### Postgraduate entry qualifications

For language requirements, please visit our [English Language Requirements page](https://www.winchester.ac.uk/international/english-language-requirements/).

All students applying for a Masters degree (MA/MSc/MBA) at Winchester should hold oneof the following qualifications:

- Bachelor degree (Honours)
- Applicants without a first degree but with 5-7 years relevant work experience may also be considered on an individual basis for some Masters programmes

Or

- Holders of the Título/Grado de Licenciado/ with a score of 4.0 or better will be considered for entry to postgraduate level study

### Visa Requirements

Students from Argentina would normally require a visa to study in the UK. [For more information please click here](https://www.winchester.ac.uk/study/international/visa-information/)

### Australia

The University of Winchester welcomes students from Australia onto a range of courses. All information below relates specifically to Australian students.

### Undergraduate

We require a High School Certificate (specific state qualifications named below) and an appropriate ATAR score of at least 70.00 or an Overall Position (Queensland) of 14. Students without an ATAR score may still be considered and should contact us to enquire.

- Capital Territory: Australia Capital Territory Year 12 Certificate
- New South Wales: Higher School Certificate (HSC)
- Northern Territory: Northern Territory Certificate of Education (NTCE, Post 2012 Northern Territory Certificate of Education and Training (NTCET)
- Queensland: Queensland Certificate of Education (QCE)
- South Australia: South Australian Certificate of Education (SACE)
- Tasmania: Tasmanian Certificate of Education (TCE)
- Victoria: Victorian Certificate of Education (VCE)
- Western Australia: Western Australia Certificate of Education (WACE)
- We also accept students with a two year Associate Diploma with good grades. Please contact us for course specific grade requirements.

### Postgraduate

- 4 Year university degree with a 2:2 or equivalent score (Division 2 / Division B / 60%)
- 3 year Bachelor degree (Ordinary)
- Applicants without a first degree but with 5 years relevant work experience may be considered for some postgraduate programmes

### English Language

Students who have studied in Australia are usually not required to submit additional evidence of English Language.
If you have not studied the full duration of High School or University in Australia you may be required to provide evidence of your English Language. Further details on this can be found if you [click here](https://www.winchester.ac.uk/international/english-language-requirements/).
